### [[The Fall of Man]].md

## Second Anniversary Q&A

-   [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2022-09-08.mp3#t=09:25) [[Fr. Stephen]] explains that redeemed humans and angels will not fall again, but for different reasons. He clarifies that Adam was created in a state of "innocence and immaturity," not perfection, which made him "mutable" and able to fall. In contrast, the saints are described as "the spirits of the righteous made perfect," having completed their journey and reached their *telos*, a state different from Adam's original condition.
-   [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2022-09-08.mp3#t=54:36) [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2022-09-08.mp3#t=58:00)" [[God]]'s command was a protection for them in their immaturity. After the fall, the human *nous* is even more clouded, "cracked and covered in mud," making correct discernment of the good much more difficult without divine grace.
