## Episode Summary

This episode is a pre-recorded Q&A session where [[Fr. Andrew]] and [[Fr. Stephen]] answer a wide variety of listener questions submitted via SpeakPipe. Topics include the nature of episcopal authority and obedience, especially in cases of heresy like iconoclasm; the large census numbers in the book of [[Numbers]]; the discovery and authority of the Codex Sinaiticus; the identity of Moloch and the nature of pagan rituals; the definition of "martyr" versus "passion-bearer" in the context of the Romanovs; and the distinction between [[Christ]]'s unique sinlessness and the sinlessness attributed to the Theotokos and St. [[John]] the Forerunner.
