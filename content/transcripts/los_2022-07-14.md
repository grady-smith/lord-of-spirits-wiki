## Recap: Creation in [[Genesis]]

To understand the [[Fall]], it is necessary to recap key themes from [[Genesis]] 1 and 2.

1.  **Creation as a [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2022-07-14.mp3#t=05:14) The creation account in [[Genesis]] 1 follows the general pattern of ancient Near East [[temple]] construction narratives. Creation is depicted as a [[temple]], and on the seventh day (the Sabbath), [[God]] is enthroned within it. This act is not one of rest from weariness but of enthronement and rule.
2.  **Order and Life**: [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2022-07-14.mp3#t=06:09) The six days of creation show a pattern: the first three days involve bringing order to chaos, and the next three days involve filling that ordered creation with life. The Garden of [[Eden]] is presented as the perfect combination of this divine order and life.
3.  **Humanity's Vocation**: [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2022-07-14.mp3#t=06:44) This pattern of ordering and filling is then handed to humanity as their core vocation: to "fill the earth and subdue it." They are to extend the order and life of the Garden to the rest of creation.
4.  **Creation of Angelic Beings**: [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2022-07-14.mp3#t=10:40) The invisible creation, including angelic beings, is part of the same creative act described in [[Genesis]] 1. The Church Fathers generally place the creation of [[angels]] within the six days, often on Day 1 (creation of light) or Day 4 (creation of heavenly bodies). This understanding contrasts with ideas like that found in Milton's *Paradise Lost*, which posits a war in heaven *before* the [[Genesis]] creation. [[Genesis]] 1 is about the creation of *everything*, visible and invisible.
