# [[1 Enoch]] 48:4-5

**[[Lord of Spirits]] Podcast Introduction**

The podcast opens with a direct quote from this passage, which describes a messianic figure who will be a staff for the righteous and the light of the nations. This figure is worshiped by all on earth, and the quote concludes by identifying the object of this worship as "the [[Lord of Spirits]]," the title of the show itself.

> [He will be a staff for the righteous with which for them to stand and not to fall, and he will be the light of the nations and the hope of those whose hearts are troubled. All who dwell on the earth will fall down and worship him, and they will praise and bless and celebrate with song the Lord of Spirits.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-04-23.mp3#t=00:03)

This passage is foundational to the podcast's exploration of the spiritual realm and its connection to [[Targums - Enochic Literature]] and the [[Divine Council - Unseen Realm]].

## [[Lord of Spirits]] Podcast Introduction

The introduction to the podcast explains that its title, "[[Lord of Spirits]]," is drawn directly from the Enochic literature. [Fr. Andrew Stephen Damick states that this title for Yahweh is a direct parallel to the Old Testament title "Lord of Hosts."](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-04-23.mp3#t=01:34) This connection highlights the continuity between the worldview of [[Second Temple]] Judaism, which produced works like [[1 Enoch]], and the canonical Old and New Testaments.

The opening of every episode features a quote from [[Book of Enoch#1 Enoch 48:4-5]], solidifying the show's thematic foundation in this ancient literature. [The quote identifies the "Lord of Spirits" as the one whom all on earth will worship.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-04-23.mp3#t=00:13) This establishes the podcast's focus on the [[Divine Council - Unseen Realm]] and the worship due to [[God]] as the ruler of that realm.

## [[Lord of Spirits]] Podcast Introduction

The podcast introduction establishes a direct link between the Enochic title [[Targums - Enochic Literature#Lord of Spirits Podcast Introduction|Lord of Spirits]] and the biblical title "Lord of Hosts" ([[Yahweh]] Sabaoth). [Fr. Andrew Stephen Damick explains this parallel, noting that "Lord of Hosts" is also rendered "Lord of Sabaoth" and sometimes translated "Lord of Armies."](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-04-23.mp3#t=01:42) This sets up the show's core premise: understanding [[God]]'s relationship with the spiritual world and the members of His heavenly council.

[[Fr. Stephen De Young]] expands on the identity of the "hosts" or "spirits." He clarifies that this term refers not only to the angelic beings but also includes the human beings who have been glorified and joined the heavenly assembly. [These hosts are the angelic spirits, but also the human beings who have joined the heavenly hosts, the saints.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-04-23.mp3#t=01:50) The introduction posits that the created world is not separate from this realm but is, in fact, "suffused with their presence," a key theme of the podcast.
