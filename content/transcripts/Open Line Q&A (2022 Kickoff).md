## Episode Summary

This is the first episode of 2022, a live call-in show where [[Fr. Andrew]] and [[Fr. Stephen]] answer a wide range of listener questions. Topics include the spiritual significance of St. Christopher's iconography, the relationship between [[Leviathan]] and the Roman Empire in the Book of [[Revelation]], the interchangeable use of *agape* and *phileo* in St. [[John]]'s Gospel, the nature of demonic hierarchy, whether marriage continues in the age to come, the modern crisis of truth, the identity of the "spirits in prison" in [[1 Peter]] 3, and the power of prayer for the departed.

## Prayer for the Departed

**Caller (Dina):** [Listen from 1:03:32](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2025-09-16.mp3#t=1:03:32)
What happens when a non-believer passes away and a believer then prays for that person? Can they come to know [[God]] after death through prayers? And the second question is, is it common for loved ones who have passed on to manifest in some way to those that are left behind and have it not be like a demonic experience?

**[[Fr. Andrew]]:** [Listen from 1:04:15](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2025-09-16.mp3#t=1:04:15)
You never know exactly what is going on in someone's heart, especially in their last hours and days... We don't know the state of their soul. We don't know exactly what's going on. But then also... other people can repent for your benefit. Right? That is why we pray for our departed. We pray that [[God]] would have mercy on them. We do the things to draw close to [[Christ]] and to advance in holiness, and we do it specifically remembering our loved ones. Not because we think that that sort of cancels out however they happen to live their life or we think that we can shoulder all of it for them or whatever, but simply this is what the Body of [[Christ]] does, and we do have an effect on each other's salvation.

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [Listen from 1:05:07](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2025-09-16.mp3#t=1:05:07)
[[God]] works through means. [[God]] can do anything he wants directly anytime he wants, but out of his love for us, he chooses to work through us... He chooses to show his love and mercy to people by us showing love and kindness and mercy to them. And he chooses to save people through our prayers. We end every service with, "Through the prayers of our holy fathers, have mercy on us and save us." Right? So [[God]] is very much capable of saving your father through your prayers and the prayers of your family. And so part of saying that prayer is offering those prayers to [[God]]... you get to be a part of that. Out of his love for you, he's given you the ability to be a part of that. And so that's how I think to think about it. In terms of seeing a departed loved one, that literally happens all the time. Just people don't want to talk about it because everybody will think they're crazy... The way to judge what is going on there is by the fruits it produces. If it sows fear and confusion and sets you back in your own repentance or causes you to be less vigilant... but if it's an experience that brings peace and love and the joy of [[Christ]], then it's something to be thankful for.
