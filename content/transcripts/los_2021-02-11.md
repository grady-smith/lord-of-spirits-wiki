### The Pattern of Ancient [[Sacrifice]]

**[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=51:56:00). And so the way that a that hospitality is going to be offered to a god and some kind of fellowship and communion with that spirit is going to take place is going to be in the form of sharing a meal with it, primarily. And so that's what sacrifice is. And so this procession sort of begins the process of doing this. And so in these processions, the practical side of the procession is you have these elements that are going to be sacrificed.

**[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=51:22:00). It could be a temple, it could be a grove... but it's a designated spot, and it's because this spot has a spiritual significance. There's been actual experiences there.

**[[Fr. Stephen De Young]]:**
The steps of the ritual typically followed this pattern:
1.  **Procession:** A festive procession to the sacred site, carrying the sacrificial elements (animals, cakes, wine, oil), accompanied by music, singing, and incense. [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=53:24:00).
2.  **Sanctification:** The elements are designated as holy for the sacrifice, often through the laying on of hands. In the case of animals, there was a ritual token to show they were "willing participants." [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=01:02:50:00).
3.  **Killing and Blood Collection:** If an animal was sacrificed, its throat was slit to drain the blood, which was seen as the life. [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=01:05:58:00). Often, this blood was poured out at the graves of heroes to "feed" their shades, as seen in Homer's *Odyssey*.
4.  **Prothesis (Preparation):** The sacrificial elements were taken to a preparation table, the *prothesis*. Here, the animal would be butchered and divided into portions for the god(s), the priests, and the people. [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=01:09:00:00).
5.  **Elevation and Offering:** [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=01:12:24:00). The portion for the god(s) was then immolated—burnt up on the altar. The pleasing aroma of the smoke rising to the heavens was how the deity consumed the offering.
6.  **The Feast:** The remaining portions of the sacrifice, along with side dishes called *trapezamata* ("table things"), were consumed by the priests and the people in a great feast. [Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2021-02-11.mp3#t=01:13:39:00).
