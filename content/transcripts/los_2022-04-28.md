# Blessings and Curses


In this episode, [[Fr. Andrew]] and [[Fr. Stephen]] delve into the biblical understanding of blessings and curses. They explore how these concepts are not about receiving "good stuff" or "bad juju," but are relational states of being—either in harmony with [[God]] and His creation (blessedness) or in a state of alienation and corruption (cursedness). They discuss the [[Day of Atonement]], [[Christ]] becoming a curse for us on the [[Cross]], and how true blessedness is found in participating in the divine life, a reality we enter into through the life of the Church.
