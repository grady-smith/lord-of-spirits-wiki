## Episode Summary

This is a pre-recorded Q&A episode where [[Fr. Andrew]] and [[Fr. Stephen]] answer a wide variety of listener questions submitted via Speak Pipe. Topics include the Christian approach to Passover seders, the meaning of Solomon's temple dedication prayer, patristic views on dancing, the prophecy about the throne in Elam from [[Jeremiah]], the scope of salvation, the veneration of saints for specific needs, distinguishing the work of the [[Holy Spirit]] from angels, the nature of Gideon's ephod, reproductive technology, and the possibility of mutual recognition of saints between the Orthodox and Roman Catholic Churches.
