# The [[Ur]] of [[Abraham]]

## An Overview of the Episode

This is the first anniversary episode of the [[Lord of Spirits]] podcast. [[Fr. Andrew]] and [[Fr. Stephen]] discuss the life and world of the patriarch [[Abraham]], placing him in his historical context of the 21st century BC, during the [[Ur]] III period of Mesopotamia. They explore the promises made by God to [[Abraham]], interpreting them not just quantitatively (many descendants) but qualitatively (descendants who would be like the stars, i.e., divine beings) and territorially (displacing the fallen principalities). The episode culminates in a detailed typological reading of the sacrifice of [[Isaac]] on Mount Moriah, framing it as a prefigurement of [[Christ]]'s own death, resurrection, and marriage to the Church.
