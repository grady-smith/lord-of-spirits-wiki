# 1 Enoch 48:4-5

**[[Lord of Spirits]] Podcast Introduction**

The podcast opens with a direct quote from this passage, which describes a messianic figure who will be a staff for the righteous and the light of the nations. This figure is worshiped by all on earth, and the quote concludes by identifying the object of this worship as "the [[Lord of Spirits]]," the title of the show itself.
