Stephen]] discusses the separation of the offices of priest and king in the Old Covenant, which was unique in the ancient world. He is given a little felonion (a priestly vestment), is permitted to cense at the Great Entrance, and receives communion with the clergy at the altar, even though he cannot perform the sacraments himself. This shows how the Christian emperor's office was integrated into the liturgical life of the Church.
