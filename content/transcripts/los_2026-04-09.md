## Prolegomena: On Harmonization

[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2026-04-09.mp3#t=03:40)

**[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2026-04-09.mp3#t=04:38). In the service of the 12 Passion gospels, to a certain extent, it's going through each [[gospel]] separately, but not always. There are a few that are composite. And then in the services for Great and Holy Friday, they are composited.

**[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2026-04-09.mp3#t=05:37). Harmonization is the primary thing that we're rejecting in favor of how we're doing it. Harmonization is when you take the accounts from the four gospels and you just kind of smush them all together to make one big composite account, but it has pieces of all four accounts jumbled together. The core problem of that is that we have four versions of the story of [[Christ]]'s death that are canonical. That means there are four versions of that story that have authority in the church. When you do your new harmonized version, you are telling a fifth version of the story. That fifth version that you came up with to try to harmonize them is not canonical. You have created a fifth [[gospel]], essentially, which is a non-canonical [[gospel]].

[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2026-04-09.mp3#t=11:54)

**[[Fr. Stephen]] De Young:** The four canonical [[gospel]] accounts all claim to be or be based on eyewitness testimony. They do not all claim to be eyewitness testimony. St. [[John]] Chrysostom pointed this out about the gospels at the end of the fourth century. He says, "Anybody who's ever investigated a matter knows that if all the witnesses give exactly the same testimony, it means they concocted their story." When you tell that story later, there is another level that enters in of conscious choices because you are choosing to tell the story a certain way to emphasize certain things about it. The [[gospel]] writers were not trying to record every single fact that they were aware of about [[Christ]]'s trials and death. They're making conscious choices, and the four [[gospel]] writers make different choices.

**[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2026-04-09.mp3#t=17:46). But also, there are elements of choices they're making that are based on who their audience is.

**[[Fr. Stephen]] De Young:** So the text... its purpose is not to assert that this happened. At the time the text were written, and at the time the text were first read, there was no doubt in anyone's mind that these things actually happened. The [[gospel]] writers are talking to us about why it's important that they happened. Claims about the significance of an event... Those don't contradict each other. That's additive. They can all be true. Having four accounts is a feature, not a bug. This gives us a greater and broader understanding of [[Christ]]'s death than just one account would.
