### The Concept of an Age in Scripture

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [So we're going to start with the whole concept of an age in Scripture because ultimately what the millennium is is an age.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=06:31) And so before we can get to that particular age, we have to understand the concept of an age in general.

[The most common way in which an age is referred to in the Hebrew Bible is with the term "day," *yom*.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=07:02) When you see "day" with the definite article, *hayom* or "the day," that is in most cases a reference to the Day of the Lord, the Day of [[Yahweh]], when [[God]] shows up and puts everything right.

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [This is true, by the way, though we're not going to go too deep into this now, this is true also in [[Genesis]] 3.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=08:26) There's that weird phrase that gets translated in all kinds of weird, contradictory ways in English, is like "the heat of the day," right? [[God]] comes walking in "the heat of the day" or "the wind of the day" or "the cool of the afternoon." What it literally says there is *ruach hayom*, the spirit of the day. And [[God]] comes walking after Adam and Eve have transgressed, and he comes walking in the spirit of the day. So this is deliberately in the Hebrew evoking the idea of judgment.

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [You also get "in that day," which can be retrospective, talking about the past, or it can be prospective, referring to the future.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=10:32) You also get "the days of" fill-in-the-blank. So, "the days of Noah" is a very common one in scripture, to refer back to the days of Noah, meaning the period when Noah was alive.

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [In Greek, what we generally have for ages is *aion*, from which we get the English word "eon".](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=17:17) And *aion* is usually literally translated "age." The word *aion* includes more than just the concept of a division of time. Ultimately, it has the resonance of a particular order, a particular state of things that prevails for a period of time.

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [This then, in Latin translation, is where we get into the word *saeculum*.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=21:02) St. Augustine really develops the way the word *saeculum* is used in his *City of [[God]]*. He uses the sort of Platonic schema of being and becoming and applies it to the city of [[God]]... and then on the other hand, on the earth, there is this constant sort of churning and turning of one *saeculum*, one order, one world, into another.

**[[Fr. Andrew]]:** [Which is then why... I don't remember exactly how the phrase goes in Latin, is it *saeculum saeculorum*?](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=23:05) In some of the early modern English translations becomes "world without end."

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [Right, because of the idea that it's a world without end versus these worlds upon worlds, one world after another, that characterizes this world in the Augustinian conception.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=23:37)

### The Ages of History

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [So there are a bunch of ages that are talked about in the scriptures. Most of these are in the Old Testament period.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=30:03)
1.  **Creation**: [The first one is sort of creation, sort of [[Genesis]] 1, before humans are created.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=31:10) This is an age which sort of has no beginning because we can't really wrap our head around it.
2.  **Paradise**: [The next age is paradise, so humans are created... This is the time in paradise before humanity is expelled from paradise.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=36:04) Humans experience time in some way, but it doesn't mean that they experienced it the same way we do now. It's a different age; it's a different world.
3.  **Pre-Flood Age**: [The expulsion from paradise is this transitional moment... to now this new age, this new era, this new world comes into being, which is the world outside of paradise leading up to the flood.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=40:41) So everything with the [[Nephilim - Giants]], everything with Lamech and his wives, and the spread of sin.
4.  **Post-Flood Age**: [The flood comes, this is our big transition event again, and the flood is very deliberately described in terms of the un-creation of the world.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=41:43) And then the world is recreated, but it's a new order. And so this new age begins after the flood, leading up to [[Abraham]], meaning we're talking about what happens with the Tower of Babel.
5.  **The Age of the [[Torah]]**: [The giving of the [[Torah]] at [[Sinai]]... the critical age in the Old Testament, because really [[Genesis]] and all these ages we've been discussing, [[Genesis]] is the prologue to the [[Torah]], which begins in [[Exodus]].](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=43:32) This order is established when the [[Torah]] is given. That age ends when Judah goes into exile.
6.  **The Great Tribulation**: [The return from exile then initiates this new age, this post-exilic age, this post-exilic period, which [[Daniel]] refers to as the Great Tribulation.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=45:25) This period is when what's left of Israel is not in control of their own land, they're under the oppression of foreign [[powers]].
7.  **The Messianic Age**: [The idea is that this great tribulation is going to be ended when the Messiah comes to deliver his people. When the Messiah comes to deliver Israel, that's going to be the hinge point again that's going to inaugurate the next age.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2024-06-27.mp3#t=47:40)

### Kiliasm / Chiliasm

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [So what we're mainly going to be talking about in the third half is Kiliasm.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:01:35:35) Kiliasm, as we're going to explain, is not exactly premillennialism, but Kiliasm is a category of views in early Christianity, second, third century, named from *kilioi* which means a thousand, that believed that that Messianic age was in the future.

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [It's really grounded originally in the kind of thinking that led the [[Pharisees]] within [[Second Temple]] Judaism to hold to the bodily [[resurrection]].](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:01:38:48) They see affirming Kiliasm, affirming this future material Messianic age in this world, as being the key to rejecting [[Gnosticism]], the key to establishing the justice of [[God]], the truth of [[God]]'s promises in the same kind of way that the [[Pharisees]] did.

### Was St. Irenaeus a Kiliast?

**[[Fr. Andrew]]:** [There's a bunch of different quotes from St. Irenaeus's great work, *Against Heresies*, that are often put forward as evidence for his supposed Kiliasm.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:02:24:19)

**[[Fr. Andrew]]:** [First one... "Inasmuch, therefore, as the opinions of certain people are derived from heretical discourses, they are both ignorant of [[God]]'s dispensations and of the mystery of the [[resurrection]] of the just and of the kingdom, which is the commencement of incorruption, by means of which kingdom those who shall be worthy are accustomed gradually to partake of the divine nature."](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:02:24:35)

**[[Fr. Andrew]]:** [Next one... "If any, however, shall endeavor to allegorize prophecies of this kind, they will not be found consistent with themselves in all points and will be confuted by the teaching of the very expressions in question."](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:02:26:27)

**[[Fr. Andrew]]:** [Here's another one... "The present world would not be annihilated, though the present corruptible state would give way to an incorruptible state where nothing grows old or loses its freshness. Those that produce a hundredfold would go up to heaven, those that produce sixtyfold to paradise, those that produce thirtyfold to the city."](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:02:30:38)

**[[Fr. Andrew]]:** [The elders who saw [[John]], the disciple of the Lord, related that they had heard from him how the Lord used to teach in regard to those times and say, "The days will come in which vines shall grow, each having 10,000 branches, and in each branch 10,000 twigs..." (and so on with extravagant numbers for grapes, wine, and wheat).](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:02:33:00)

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [This is a reference to another text. This is a reference to 1 [[Enoch]] 10:19. And in 1 [[Enoch]], this is talking about the Messianic age.

**[[Fr. Stephen]]:** [So all of that having been laid out, it's pretty obvious what St. [[John]] is talking about here in [[Revelation]] 20.](https://media.ancientfaith.com/audio/podcasts/lordofspirits_208.mp3#t:02:37:31) The binding of the [[devil]] is another way of talking about what happens in [[Daniel]] when the dominion is stripped away from the beasts and given to the [[Son of Man]] who is enthroned. This is correcting a contrary view, that these people have been martyred, they have suffered for [[Christ]], these people are dead, surely they must be of some lesser caliber than those who will still be alive at the time that [[Christ]] has his glorious appearing.
