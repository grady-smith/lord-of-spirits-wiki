# Demonic Activity

## Introduction and Episode Summary

[Listen from here](https://media.ancientfaith.com/lordofspirits/los_2025-02-13.mp3#t=0:00) ([[1 Enoch]] 48:4-5)

This episode focuses on [[Demons|demonic activity]], covering a spectrum from temptation and torment to outright possession. [[Fr. Andrew]] and [[Fr. Stephen]] begin by reviewing foundational concepts from previous episodes, defining what a [[Spirits|spirit]] is in the ancient context and how the term '[[Demons|demon]]' (`daimon`) evolved from a neutral pagan term to its negative Christian connotation. They explore the nature of thoughts as external phenomena perceived by the mind (`nous`) and distinguish this from the formulation of ideas, which is an act of the will. This framework is essential for understanding spiritual warfare. The episode also discusses the various angelic rebellions mentioned in Scripture and tradition, the nature of exorcism, and the different levels of demonic influence, culminating in what they term "participation," where a human being fully synergizes with a demon's will. Callers ask about the resurrection of the body, the nature of ghosts, and the spiritual significance of mythological creatures like ghouls. The fathers conclude by emphasizing that the ordinary, faithful practice of the Christian life—prayer, worship, fasting, and charity—is itself exorcistic, as it fills the "house" of the soul with God's presence, leaving no room for evil.
