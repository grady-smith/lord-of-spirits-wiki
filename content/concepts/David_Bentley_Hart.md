title: "David Bentley Hart"
tags: [concept]

David Bentley Hart is a contemporary Eastern Orthodox theologian, philosopher, and cultural critic, known for his dense, erudite prose and his forceful translation of the New Testament. While not a historical or ancient figure, his work frequently intersects with the themes of ancient Christian cosmology, metaphysics, and the critique of modernity, making him a notable figure in modern theological discourse.

In discussions related to the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and broader Orthodox theology, Hart is often referenced for his staunch defense of classical theism against modern materialist and deist conceptions of God. He strongly advocates for Christian universalism (apokatastasis), a view that is heavily debated within Orthodoxy. However, his writings align well with the podcast’s emphasis on defeating the mechanical, secularized view of the cosmos, insisting instead on an enchanted universe where God is the foundational source of all being and reality.
