---

Paul (formerly Saul of Tarsus) was a zealous Pharisee who intensely persecuted the early Church until his dramatic encounter with the risen Christ on the road to Damascus. He subsequently became the "Apostle to the Gentiles," writing much of the New Testament and establishing churches throughout the Roman Empire.

The *Lord of Spirits* podcast frequently highlights how deeply Paul's theology is rooted in Second Temple Judaism and the concept of the Divine Council. When Paul speaks of "principalities, powers, rulers of the darkness of this age, and spiritual hosts of wickedness in the heavenly places" (Ephesians 6:12), he is not using abstract metaphors for human governments; he is directly referencing the rebellious angelic beings that rule the pagan nations.

Paul’s entire mission to the Gentiles is understood as an act of cosmic warfare. By preaching the Gospel and baptizing Gentiles into the Church, Paul was actively plundering the domains of the fallen gods, bringing the nations back into the inheritance of Yahweh. His epistles meticulously explain how the death and resurrection of Christ defeated these hostile powers, uniting Jews and Gentiles into one new humanity within the Body of Christ.
