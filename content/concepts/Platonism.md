---
title: "Platonism"
tags: [concept]
---

Platonism is the philosophical system derived from the teachings of Plato and his followers, centered around the theory of Forms. It posits that the physical world is merely a shadow or imperfect reflection of a higher, eternal, and unchanging spiritual reality. This philosophy profoundly influenced the intellectual climate of the Hellenistic period and Late Antiquity, intersecting deeply with Second Temple Judaism and early Christian theology.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, Platonism is frequently engaged to explain how early Christians articulated the relationship between the uncreated God and the created order. While Platonism provided useful vocabulary (such as *Logos* and *nous*) for expressing complex theological truths, Orthodox Christianity fundamentally subverted Platonism's negative view of matter. The Incarnation of Jesus Christ—the Uncreated Word taking on physical flesh—shattered the Platonic divide, affirming that the material world is not a trap to escape, but a creation meant to participate in divine life through transfiguration.
