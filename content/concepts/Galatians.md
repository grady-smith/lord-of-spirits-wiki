title: "Galatians"

---

The Epistle to the Galatians is a letter written by the Apostle Paul to a group of early Christian communities in the Roman province of Galatia. The letter addresses a critical crisis: some Jewish-Christian teachers (often called "Judaizers") were insisting that Gentile converts must be circumcised and adhere to the Mosaic Law to be fully justified and part of God's covenant people.

In Orthodox theology, Galatians is central to understanding the nature of justification and the new covenant. Paul vehemently argues that salvation comes through faithfulness to Jesus Christ, not by the "works of the law." In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, Paul's arguments underscore that the coming of Christ fundamentally altered the cosmic landscape. The Law served as a temporary guardian, but through Christ, believers (both Jew and Gentile) are adopted as mature sons and heirs, freed from the elemental spiritual forces of the world.
