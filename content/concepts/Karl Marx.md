---
title: "Karl Marx"
tags: [concept]
---

Karl Marx was a 19th-century philosopher and economist whose materialist worldview profoundly shaped modern secular thought. He viewed religion as the "opiate of the masses" and sought to explain all of history through economic and class struggles.

The podcast often contrasts the strictly materialist and disenchanted worldview of thinkers like Marx with the deeply spiritual and enchanted cosmology of the ancient Christians, where unseen spiritual forces are the primary drivers of human history.
