The Epistle to the Ephesians is a letter written by the Apostle Paul to the Christian community in Ephesus, a major commercial and religious hub in the Roman Empire, famously home to the massive Temple of Artemis. The letter focuses heavily on the cosmic implications of Christ’s resurrection and ascension, the unity of Jewish and Gentile believers in the Church, and the necessity of spiritual armor for the believer.

For Orthodox theology and *The Lord of Spirits*, Ephesians is a paramount text regarding spiritual warfare and cosmic geography. In Ephesians 6, Paul explicitly defines the Christian struggle not as a battle against "flesh and blood" (other humans or political structures), but against the "rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places." This confirms the ancient worldview that the world is actively contested by fallen members of the Divine Council, and that the Church’s existence as the unified body of Christ is itself a declaration of victory over these demonic principalities.
