---
title: "Book of Jubilees"
tags: [concept]
---

The *Book of Jubilees* is an ancient Jewish pseudepigraphal text from the Second Temple period, often considered canonical by the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church. Functioning as a form of "rewritten Bible," it presents a detailed expansion of the Genesis and Exodus narratives, ostensibly revealed to Moses by angels on Mount Sinai. The text divides history into periods of jubilees (49-year cycles) and is characterized by its strict solar calendar and intense emphasis on ritual purity, sabbath observance, and the avoidance of Gentile defilement.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* and biblical cosmology, *Jubilees* is vital for its elaboration on the demonic realm. It explicitly links the origin of demons to the disembodied spirits of the Nephilim (the offspring of the Watchers and human women). According to *Jubilees*, after the Flood destroyed the physical bodies of the giants, their spirits were allowed by God—upon the petition of the chief demon Mastema—to remain on earth to tempt, afflict, and corrupt humanity until the final judgment. This theological framework heavily influenced the early Christian understanding of spiritual warfare and the necessity of exorcism.
