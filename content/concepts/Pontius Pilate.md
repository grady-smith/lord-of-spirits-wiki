---
title: "Pontius Pilate"
tags: [concept]
---

Pontius Pilate was the fifth prefect of the Roman province of Judaea, serving under Emperor Tiberius from approximately AD 26 to 36. In the biblical narrative, he is the Roman official who presided over the trial of Jesus Christ and ultimately ordered his crucifixion, despite finding no legitimate political treason to warrant the execution. 

Within the theological framework discussed in the *Lord of Spirits*, Pilate represents the intersection of the earthly empire with the spiritual powers of darkness. His capitulation to the mob and execution of the innocent Christ is the pivotal moment where the rulers of this age—both visible and invisible—orchestrate what they believe is the defeat of God's Son. However, as articulated by the Apostle Paul, had these rulers known the hidden wisdom of God, they would not have crucified the Lord of Glory. Pilate’s decree inadvertently serves as the catalyst for Christ's descent into Hades and the ensuing cosmic victory over death.
