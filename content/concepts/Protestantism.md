title: "Protestantism"
tags: [concept]
---

Protestantism is one of the major branches of Western Christianity, originating in the 16th-century Reformation led by figures like Martin Luther and John Calvin. It encompasses a wide variety of denominations and theological traditions, but is generally united by a rejection of the authority of the Pope and an emphasis on the individual believer's direct access to God through the scriptures.

Through the lens of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Protestantism is often analyzed as a movement that attempted to reform medieval Western errors but ultimately retained many of their underlying philosophical assumptions, particularly nominalism and legalistic soteriology. While affirming the Protestant desire to return to the biblical text, the podcast frequently highlights how the loss of the ancient liturgical context, the reduction of the sacraments, and the marginalization of the unseen realm resulted in a faith that struggles to fully engage with the cosmic and sacramental worldview of the early Church.
