title: "Cyrus"

---
Cyrus, commonly known as Cyrus the Great, was the founder of the Achaemenid Persian Empire. In the 6th century BC, he conquered the Neo-Babylonian Empire, which had previously destroyed Jerusalem and exiled the Jewish people. Cyrus is renowned in the biblical narrative for issuing a decree that allowed the exiled Jews to return to Judah and rebuild the Temple in Jerusalem. 

In the ancient Near Eastern context and biblical theology, Cyrus is remarkably referred to by the prophet Isaiah as Yahweh’s "messiah" or "anointed one" (Isaiah 45:1). This is significant because it demonstrates Yahweh's sovereign authority over all nations and their rulers, even those who do not explicitly worship Him. From the perspective of *The Lord of Spirits*, God’s use of a pagan king to accomplish the deliverance and restoration of His people underscores that the gods of the nations (the fallen angelic beings) are powerless before Yahweh, who directs human history to fulfill His redemptive purposes.
