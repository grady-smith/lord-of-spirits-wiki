title: "Apocalypse"
tags: [concept]
---
In modern parlance, "apocalypse" has come to mean a catastrophic end-of-the-world event. However, in the ancient Near Eastern context and biblical literature, the Greek word *apokalypsis* literally means "unveiling" or "revelation." It refers to the pulling back of the curtain that separates the visible, material world from the unseen, spiritual realm. 

As explored in the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, apocalyptic literature—such as Daniel, 1 Enoch, and the Book of Revelation—does not merely predict future disasters; it reveals what is happening right now from a heavenly perspective. It shows the earthly struggles of God's people as the visible manifestation of a massive, ongoing cosmic war between the Divine Council of Yahweh and the rebellious spiritual powers. When a prophet receives an apocalypse, he is granted a vision of the divine throne room and the true spiritual realities underlying human history.

Therefore, an apocalypse is profoundly hopeful rather than fatalistic. By unveiling the hidden reality, it assures the faithful that despite the apparent dominance of evil empires and pagan gods on earth, the Most High God is firmly on His throne. It reveals that the victory of Christ over death and the demonic powers is already a reality in the heavenly places, and will ultimately be fully consummated on earth.
