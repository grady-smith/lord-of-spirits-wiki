title: "Rabbinic Judaism"
tags: [concept]

Rabbinic Judaism is the normative form of Judaism that developed after the destruction of the Second Temple in AD 70. Without a temple, priesthood, or sacrificial system, the religion was forced to adapt, shifting its focus to the study of the Torah, the authority of the rabbis, and the development of the Oral Law, eventually codified in the Mishnah and the Talmud.

Within the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, Rabbinic Judaism is not seen as the direct, unchanged continuation of ancient Israelite religion, but rather as one of two major surviving branches of Second Temple Judaism—the other being Christianity. The podcast frequently emphasizes that Rabbinic Judaism actively redefined itself in opposition to the early Christian Church, systematically rejecting the ancient theology of the divine council, the "Two Powers in Heaven," and the rich apocalyptic tradition that originally characterized the worldview of the patriarchs and prophets.
