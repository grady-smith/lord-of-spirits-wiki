---
title: "King David"
tags: [concept]
---

King David is the archetypal king of Israel, an earthly counterpart to the heavenly reign of Yahweh. He captured Jerusalem, centralized the worship of God, and received the Davidic covenant promising an eternal throne.

David's military campaigns are often viewed as spiritual warfare, subduing the nations and their patron gods under the rule of Yahweh. He is a primary typological figure of Christ, the ultimate King who conquers the spiritual powers of death and Hades.
