---
title: "Marduk"
tags: [concept]
---

Marduk was the patron god of Babylon and a chief deity in the Mesopotamian pantheon. In the Enuma Elish (the Babylonian creation epic), Marduk achieves supremacy by violently defeating the chaos monster Tiamat.

In the biblical worldview, Marduk is not a myth but a real, rebellious spiritual being (a demon or fallen son of God) who usurped worship from humanity. The prophets continually mock the impotence of gods like Marduk in comparison to the true sovereignty of Yahweh.
