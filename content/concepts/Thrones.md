---
title: "Thrones"
tags: [concept]
---

In Orthodox angelology, drawn from the celestial hierarchy formalized by St. Dionysius the Areopagite, "Thrones" constitute one of the highest ranks of angelic beings, grouped alongside the Seraphim and Cherubim. They are mentioned by St. Paul in Colossians 1:16 as part of the invisible creation ("thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities"). The Thrones are traditionally understood as spiritual beings of immense purity and power through whom the justice and majesty of God are executed in the cosmos.

The *Lord of Spirits* often highlights the Thrones within the context of the Divine Council and ancient Near Eastern royal imagery. In the ancient world, a throne was not merely a piece of furniture but a symbol of cosmic authority; thus, angelic Thrones are animate, spiritual realities that directly support and convey the kingship of Yahweh. They represent the steadfast, unshakeable justice of the Most High God, serving as the foundation upon which His divine rule is enacted both in the heavens and over the nations of the earth.
