---
title: "Nahum"
tags: [concept]
---

Nahum was a minor prophet of the Old Testament whose brief book is entirely dedicated to prophesying the destruction of Nineveh, the capital of the brutal Assyrian Empire, which occurred around 612 BC. Unlike Jonah, who was sent to call Nineveh to repentance, Nahum delivers an oracle of final judgment against the city.

In the *Lord of Spirits* framework, Assyria is not merely viewed as a violent geopolitical enemy, but as an empire entirely given over to demonic powers, functioning as an avatar of chaos and violence on earth. Nineveh was a stronghold of the spiritual forces arrayed against God’s creation. Nahum’s prophecy is therefore a declaration of divine warfare, proclaiming that Yahweh is an avenging God who will ultimately destroy the tyrannical human institutions and the fallen spiritual beings that back them.

The book of Nahum emphasizes the theological reality that God’s justice is actively engaged in human history. The destruction of Nineveh is a macrocosmic example of God breaking the power of the demonic oppressors, providing a source of comfort to the faithful (the name Nahum itself means "comfort" or "consolation"). It serves as a precursor to the final eschatological defeat of all hostile powers.
