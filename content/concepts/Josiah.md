---
title: "Josiah"
tags: [concept]
---

Josiah was a righteous king of Judah who enacted massive religious reforms after discovering the Book of the Law. His reign is characterized by a violent purging of idolatry, including the destruction of the high places and the Asherah poles.

In the ancient Near Eastern context, Josiah was not simply correcting liturgical errors; he was actively dismantling the infrastructure of demonic worship. By destroying the cultic sites of the fallen sons of God, he aligned Judah back to the exclusive worship of Yahweh.
