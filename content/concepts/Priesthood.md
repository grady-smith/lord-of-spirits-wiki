---
title: "Priesthood"
tags: [concept]
---

The priesthood is the formalized institutional structure of mediation between the divine and humanity, established in ancient Israel to maintain the sanctity of the Tabernacle and later the Temple. It operated on strict rules of genealogical descent (the Aaronic line), ritual purity, and liturgical function. 

Within the theology of the *Lord of Spirits*, the priesthood represents the necessary human cooperation in maintaining sacred space. Because God is entirely holy, His presence in a fallen world is described as a consuming fire; the priesthood functions to insulate and mediate this presence so it heals rather than destroys. In the Orthodox Christian understanding, the priesthood was not abolished but transformed. Christ operates as the eternal High Priest according to the order of Melchizedek, while the Christian priesthood participates in His ongoing ministry, offering the unbloody sacrifice of the Eucharist and continuing the work of purifying and consecrating the world.
