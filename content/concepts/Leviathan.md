---
title: "Leviathan"
tags: [concept]
---

Leviathan is the ancient Near Eastern chaos dragon, representing the untamed, destructive forces of the sea and the demonic realm. In various mythologies (like the Ugaritic Lotan), it is a multi-headed serpent.

In biblical theology, Yahweh is depicted as crushing the heads of Leviathan, establishing His sovereignty over chaos. This motif is fully realized in the New Testament when Christ tramples down death by death, decisively defeating the ultimate chaos monster, the devil.
