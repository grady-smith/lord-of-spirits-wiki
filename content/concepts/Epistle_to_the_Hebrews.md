title: "Epistle to the Hebrews"
tags: [concept]
---
The Epistle to the Hebrews is a deeply theological New Testament text written to early Jewish Christians who were facing persecution and the temptation to revert to the old covenant sacrificial system. To persuade them to remain faithful, the author meticulously demonstrates how Jesus Christ is the ultimate fulfillment of, and superior to, all the institutions of the old covenant: the angels, Moses, the Levitical priesthood, and the Temple sacrifices.

In Orthodox theology and the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Hebrews is the foundational text for understanding Christ's heavenly priesthood and the cosmic nature of the Liturgy. The epistle argues that the earthly tabernacle was merely a "copy and shadow" of the heavenly reality. Christ, operating as the ultimate High Priest in the order of Melchizedek, did not enter a man-made sanctuary, but ascended into the heavenly Holy of Holies to offer His own blood. This once-for-all cosmic sacrifice purifies the heavens and provides the basis for the Orthodox understanding of the Eucharist as participation in that eternal, heavenly reality.
