---
title: "Chalice"
tags: [concept]
---

The Chalice is the sacred vessel used in the Divine Liturgy to hold the Eucharistic wine, which, through the invocation of the Holy Spirit (the *Epiclesis*), becomes the true and life-giving Blood of Jesus Christ. In Orthodox theology, the Chalice is an object of profound reverence, as it contains the ultimate remedy for mortality and the means of direct communion with the uncreated energies of God. It represents the fulfillment of the Old Testament sacrificial system, offering the true blood of the New Covenant that cleanses the cosmos.

Within the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, partaking from the Chalice is an act of cosmic participation and spiritual warfare. In the ancient world, partaking of a sacrifice meant communing with the deity to whom it was offered. By drinking from the Chalice, the faithful are physically uniting themselves to the resurrected, glorified humanity of Christ, thereby displacing the corruption of sin and death. It is an act of allegiance to the true God, serving as the ultimate counter-meal to the idolatrous feasts of the fallen gods of the nations.
