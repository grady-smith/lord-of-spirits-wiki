title: "First Clement"
tags: [concept]
---

First Clement is an early Christian epistle written around the end of the 1st century AD, traditionally attributed to Clement of Rome, a prominent bishop in the early Roman church. Addressed to the church in Corinth, the letter was occasioned by a dispute in which several presbyters had been unjustly deposed by younger members of the congregation. Clement writes to urge repentance, humility, and the restoration of order.

In the context of early Christian history and theology, First Clement is an invaluable document. It provides early evidence of the concept of apostolic succession, emphasizing that church leaders derive their authority from the apostles, who received it from Christ. The letter is deeply steeped in the Old Testament, frequently drawing on examples from ancient Israel to instruct the Corinthians, demonstrating the seamless continuity the early Christians saw between the Hebrew Scriptures and the newly established Church.
