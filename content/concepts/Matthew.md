---
title: "Matthew"
tags: [concept]
---

The Gospel of Matthew was written with a heavily Jewish audience in mind, emphasizing Jesus as the definitive fulfillment of Old Testament prophecies and the true King of Israel.

Matthew portrays Christ establishing the Kingdom of Heaven on earth, actively displacing the illegitimate rule of the fallen spiritual powers. Through teachings like the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus issues the new Torah, instructing His followers on how to live as citizens of this restored cosmic kingdom.
