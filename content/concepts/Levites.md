---
title: "Levites"
tags: [concept]
---

The Levites functioned as the lesser clergy and guardians of the sacred precincts, assisting the priests (who were specifically from the line of Aaron). 

Their role was intrinsically tied to the maintenance of ritual purity and the mechanics of sacred space. By ensuring that the sanctuary was kept holy and free from the contagion of death and sin, the Levites allowed Yahweh to safely dwell among His people without consuming them.
