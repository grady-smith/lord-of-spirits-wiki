---
title: "Resurrection"
tags: [concept]
---

The Resurrection refers to the reuniting of the soul and the physical body after death, culminating in the ultimate defeat of death itself. While hints of this exist in the older texts of the Hebrew Bible, the theology of a general bodily resurrection became highly developed during the Second Temple period, famously championed by the Pharisees and vehemently rejected by the Sadducees. 

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Resurrection of Jesus Christ is the absolute center of cosmic history. It is not simply an apologetic proof of His divinity or a mechanism for individual human salvation to "go to heaven." Instead, the Resurrection is a hostile invasion of Hades by the Author of Life. By descending into the underworld and rising again, Christ shatters the gates of Hades, plunders the realm of the dead, and defeats the spiritual powers of death and corruption. It guarantees the eventual bodily resurrection and transfiguration of all humanity.
