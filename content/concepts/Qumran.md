Qumran is an archaeological site in the Judean desert near the northwestern shore of the Dead Sea. It is most famous as the settlement of an ascetic Jewish sect, widely believed to be the Essenes, who lived there from the late second century BC until the destruction of the site by the Romans in AD 68. The nearby caves are the discovery site of the Dead Sea Scrolls.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Qumran and its library are vital for understanding the theological milieu of Second Temple Judaism during the time of Christ. The texts found at Qumran—including ancient copies of the Hebrew scriptures, the Book of Enoch, and sectarian documents detailing the "War of the Sons of Light Against the Sons of Darkness"—demonstrate that beliefs in a complex angelology, a populated divine council, and impending cosmic warfare were not later Christian inventions, but were deeply rooted in the Judaism of the period.
