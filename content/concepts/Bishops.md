Bishops represent the collective apostolic succession and the highest order of the clergy within the Orthodox Church. Gathering in synods and ecumenical councils, bishops collectively govern the Church, resolve theological disputes, and articulate dogma through the guidance of the Holy Spirit. Their conciliarity reflects the communal nature of the Holy Trinity and the collaborative structure of the Divine Council, ensuring that no single individual holds supreme, unchecked authority over the entire Church.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and early Christian cosmology, the assembly of bishops is the earthly manifestation of the heavenly council. When bishops meet in council to condemn heresy or establish canon law, they are enacting the judgment of the Most High God against the false teachings inspired by fallen spiritual powers. The unity of the bishops in the Eucharist and in doctrine is a direct repudiation of the division and chaos sowed by the demonic realm, serving to anchor the Church as the ark of salvation in a hostile world.
