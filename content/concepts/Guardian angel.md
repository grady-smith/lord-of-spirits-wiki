---
title: "Guardian angel"
tags: [concept]
---

A guardian angel is a spiritual being assigned by God to protect and guide a specific person, community, or nation. In the ancient Near Eastern and Second Temple Jewish context, the idea that spiritual beings were delegated to oversee aspects of creation was widespread, often associated with the concept of the Divine Council where the "sons of God" were appointed over the nations (Deuteronomy 32:8-9).

In Orthodox theology, the belief in guardian angels is deeply ingrained in the liturgical and spiritual life. Every baptized Christian is believed to be entrusted to a guardian angel who serves as an invisible companion, offering protection from demonic assault, encouraging repentance, and offering prayers on the believer's behalf. This understanding reinforces the view of a deeply enchanted cosmos where the physical and spiritual realms are intimately intertwined in the ongoing drama of salvation.
