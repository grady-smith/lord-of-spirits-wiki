---
title: "Day of the Lord"
tags: [concept]
---
The "Day of the Lord" is a prominent prophetic concept in the Hebrew Bible, referring to a specific, future moment in history when Yahweh will decisively intervene in the world to judge His enemies, vindicate His faithful people, and restore cosmic order. It is often depicted with terrifying, cataclysmic imagery—darkness, cosmic upheaval, and holy war. 

In the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, the Day of the Lord is not merely a political or earthly judgment, but the final, eschatological defeat of the fallen Divine Council—the rebellious gods of the nations. While early Israelite audiences often assumed this day would bring unmitigated blessing to them and destruction to their neighbors, the prophets (like Amos) warned that God's justice would begin with His own unfaithful people. For Orthodox Christians, the Day of the Lord was inaugurated by the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ, who defeated death and the demonic powers, and it will be consummated at His glorious Second Coming when all things are made new.
