---
title: "Priest"
tags: [concept]
---

A priest in the ancient world, including ancient Israel, was an appointed mediator between the divine and human realms. Functioning primarily in a temple or tabernacle setting, the priest was responsible for offering sacrifices, maintaining the ritual purity of sacred space, and mediating the presence of God to the community. In Israel, this role was exclusively assigned to the descendants of Aaron within the tribe of Levi.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the role of a priest is fundamentally understood as maintaining the intersection between heaven and earth. The priest is not just a moral instructor, but a vital liturgical operator who cleanses the community and its sacred spaces from the defilement of sin and death, preventing the withdrawal of God's presence. In the New Covenant, Jesus Christ is the ultimate High Priest, and the ordained priesthood of the Orthodox Church continues this ancient function by offering the Eucharist, which connects the earthly congregation to the eternal, heavenly liturgy.
