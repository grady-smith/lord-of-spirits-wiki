title: "Queen Mother"
tags: [concept]
---

In the ancient Near East and specifically within the Davidic monarchy of ancient Israel, the Queen Mother (or *Gebirah*, meaning "Great Lady") was a position of significant political and intercessory power. Because Israelite kings practiced polygamy, the most influential female figure in the royal court was not one of the king's many wives, but his mother. She sat on a throne at the king's right hand and acted as an intercessor for the people.

In Orthodox theology and the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the historical reality of the Davidic Queen Mother provides the biblical and typological basis for the veneration of the Virgin Mary, the Theotokos. Just as Bathsheba interceded before King Solomon, Mary is understood to sit at the right hand of her Son, Jesus Christ, the eternal Davidic King. Her intercessory role is a continuation of this ancient Israelite royal structure, elevated to the heavenly court.
