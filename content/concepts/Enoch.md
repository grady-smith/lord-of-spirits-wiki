Enoch is an enigmatic figure in the Book of Genesis, noted as the seventh generation from Adam. The biblical text famously states that "Enoch walked with God, and he was not, for God took him" (Genesis 5:24). Because he did not experience a natural death, Enoch became a central figure in ancient Jewish apocalyptic literature, most notably in the Book of 1 Enoch, where he is portrayed as a visionary who ascends to heaven and receives divine revelation regarding the cosmos, angels, and the judgment of the fallen Watchers.

In *The Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Enochic tradition is treated as essential background for understanding the New Testament. The Book of 1 Enoch (which is explicitly quoted in the Epistle of Jude) provides the detailed narrative of the rebellious angels (Watchers) who descended on Mount Hermon, corrupted humanity, and sired the giant Nephilim. Enoch’s role as the scribe of righteousness who pronounces God's judgment upon these fallen powers establishes a major theological paradigm for Christ's later earthly ministry, which the podcast frames as the ultimate defeat of these same demonic entities.
