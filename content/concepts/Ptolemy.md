Ptolemy refers to the dynasty of Macedonian Greek rulers who governed Egypt after the death of Alexander the Great in 323 BC, founded by Ptolemy I Soter. The Ptolemaic Kingdom was one of the major Hellenistic powers and controlled Judea for over a century before losing it to the rival Seleucid Empire. Their capital, Alexandria, became a primary center of Hellenistic Jewish culture and learning.

Within the context of Second Temple Judaism and the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Ptolemaic period is crucial because it was under the sponsorship of Ptolemy II Philadelphus that the Hebrew scriptures were translated into Greek, resulting in the Septuagint. This translation profoundly shaped the theological vocabulary of the ancient Jewish diaspora and subsequently became the authoritative Old Testament text for the Apostles and the early Christian Church, providing the linguistic framework for the New Testament's articulation of the divine council and the unseen realm.
