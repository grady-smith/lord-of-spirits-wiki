title: "Leviticus"
tags: [concept]
---

Leviticus is the third book of the Torah, detailing the sacrificial system, purity laws, and the duties of the priesthood. Often misunderstood as mere legalism, it is highly theological.

In the *Lord of Spirits* context, Leviticus provides the necessary ritual mechanics to maintain sacred space. The sacrifices and purity laws were designed to cleanse the community and the sanctuary from the miasma (contagion) of sin and demonic influence, allowing the holy God to commune safely with a fallen world.
