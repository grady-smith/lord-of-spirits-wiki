title: "Bobby Maddox"
tags: [concept]
---

Bobby Maddox is the station manager at Ancient Faith Radio and the audio producer/engineer for the highly popular Orthodox podcast, *The Lord of Spirits*. While not a theological concept or an ancient historical figure, Bobby is a frequent and beloved fixture within the lore of the podcast itself. Hosts Fr. Stephen De Young and Fr. Andrew Stephen Damick regularly reference him in lighthearted, ongoing inside jokes, often playfully attributing impossible feats, ancient historical presence, or cosmic interventions to him.

Beyond the humor, the frequent mention of Bobby Maddox highlights the communal and grounded nature of modern Orthodox media production. It reminds listeners that the deep dives into ancient theology, biblical cosmology, and spiritual warfare are facilitated by real people working behind the scenes. In the meta-narrative of the show's community, Bobby serves as the connective tissue bridging the ancient, unseen realm discussed by the priests and the modern, digital delivery of that content to the audience.
