---
title: "Zechariah"
tags: [concept]
---

Zechariah was a post-exilic prophet whose highly apocalyptic visions deeply influenced Second Temple Judaism and early Christian theology. His book is filled with vivid imagery of the unseen realm, including angelic mediators, the Divine Council in session, Joshua the High Priest standing before the Angel of the Lord while being accused by Satan, and prophecies concerning a messianic king riding on a donkey.

In the *Lord of Spirits* framework, Zechariah provides a crucial look at the mechanics of the spiritual world and the interactions of the Divine Council. The vision of the High Priest Joshua explicitly portrays the "Satan" not as a generic concept of evil, but as a prosecuting figure within the heavenly court whose accusations are definitively rebuked by the Angel of the Lord (the pre-incarnate Christ). Zechariah’s prophecies firmly bridge the gap between the Old Testament temple rituals and the eschatological victory of Christ over the rebellious spiritual powers.
