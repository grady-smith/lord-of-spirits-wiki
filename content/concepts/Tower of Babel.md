title: "Tower of Babel"
tags: [concept]
---

The Tower of Babel, described in Genesis 11, marks a catastrophic rebellion against God following the Flood. According to the Biblical narrative and deeply expanded upon in Second Temple Jewish literature, humanity gathered at Shinar to build a ziggurat—a temple-tower meant to bridge the heavens and the earth on human terms, essentially attempting to manipulate the divine. In response, God confounded their language and scattered humanity across the earth.

The *Lord of Spirits* podcast heavily emphasizes the crucial interpretation found in Deuteronomy 32:8-9 (specifically via the Septuagint and Dead Sea Scrolls), which states that at Babel, God divided the nations according to the number of the "sons of God." This event was essentially a divine disinheritance: God allotted the seventy nations to the governance of lesser divine beings (the divine council), reserving only Israel for Himself. These divine beings later rebelled and demanded worship as the pagan gods, establishing the primary geopolitical and spiritual warfare paradigm of the Old Testament.
