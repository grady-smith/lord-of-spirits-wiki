---
title: "Luke"
tags: [concept]
---

The Gospel of Luke presents the ministry of Jesus as a systematic, geographical reconquest of the earth from demonic forces. It heavily emphasizes Christ's authority over unclean spirits through numerous exorcisms.

A key element in Luke is the sending of the seventy (or seventy-two) disciples. This number corresponds to the seventy nations of the world in Genesis 10, signaling that Christ is beginning the process of disinheriting the fallen "sons of God" and reclaiming all nations for Himself.
