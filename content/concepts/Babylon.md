---
title: "Babylon"
tags: [concept]
---

Babylon stands in the biblical narrative not just as a historical empire, but as the archetypal symbol of human rebellion, idolatry, and anti-God civilization. Its origins trace back to the Tower of Babel, where humanity attempted to build a cosmic mountain to manipulate the divine. Historically, the Neo-Babylonian Empire, under figures like Nebuchadnezzar, was responsible for the destruction of the First Temple in Jerusalem and the devastating exile of the Jewish people in 586 BC, representing a period of profound spiritual and national crisis.

In the framework of the *Lord of Spirits* and Orthodox cosmology, Babylon is the earthly manifestation of the demonic principalities' attempt to establish a rival kingdom to Yahweh's. It is characterized by the worship of Marduk, the oppression of the weak, and the institutionalization of chaos masquerading as order. In apocalyptic literature, such as the Book of Revelation, "Babylon" becomes the ultimate eschatological symbol for the corrupt, demonically inspired world system that opposes Christ and His Church, destined for final destruction upon the Day of the Lord.
