---
title: "Pentecost"
tags: [concept]
---

Pentecost, originally known in the Hebrew Bible as the Feast of Weeks (Shavuot), was an ancient Israelite agricultural festival celebrating the first fruits of the wheat harvest. By the Second Temple period, it had also become a commemoration of the giving of the Law to Moses on Mount Sinai. It was one of the three major pilgrimage festivals that required Israelite men to gather in Jerusalem.

In the Christian tradition, Pentecost marks the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the Apostles as recorded in the Book of Acts. Within the theology of the *Lord of Spirits*, this event is understood as a pivotal reversal of the Tower of Babel. Whereas the nations were divided and placed under the authority of angelic watchers at Babel, Pentecost represents the beginning of the reclamation of the nations by Yahweh. The Apostles are empowered to speak the languages of the dispersed nations, signaling that the reign of the fallen gods has ended and the nations are being drawn back into the divine council through Christ.
