Vespers is the traditional evening prayer service in the Orthodox Church, marking the liturgical beginning of the new day (following the ancient Jewish reckoning where the day begins at sunset). It is a service heavily steeped in Psalmody, incense, and hymns, transitioning the faithful from the labor of the day into the peace of the evening. The structure of Vespers traces the history of salvation, beginning with the creation of the world (Psalm 104) and culminating in the joyous expectation of the coming of Christ, the "Gladsome Light."

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, liturgical services like Vespers are emphasized as actual participation in the heavenly reality. The offering of incense during Vespers is directly linked to the prayers of the saints ascending to the heavenly altar, as described in Revelation. Furthermore, by structuring time sanctified by prayer, Vespers serves as a daily act of spiritual warfare, reorienting the mind away from the chaotic distractions of the fallen world and aligning the believer with the harmonious rhythm of the Divine Council.
