The New Testament is the collection of 27 foundational texts of the Christian Church, consisting of the four Gospels, the Acts of the Apostles, the epistles of Paul and other apostles, and the Book of Revelation. It was written in Koine Greek in the first century AD by the early followers of Jesus Christ.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the New Testament is heavily emphasized as the organic continuation and ultimate fulfillment of the Old Testament and Second Temple Judaism. It is not the foundational text of a brand-new religion that discarded the old, but the record of how Yahweh fulfilled His covenant promises through the Incarnation of the Messiah. The New Testament writers directly utilize the cosmological worldview of their day, heavily employing the concepts of the Divine Council, spiritual warfare, and the defeat of hostile cosmic powers (principalities and powers).

The New Testament demonstrates how Jesus Christ recapitulates the history of Israel, defeats the spiritual powers that held the nations captive, and establishes the Church as the new, expanded Israel. The liturgical life and sacramental theology of the early Church described in these texts are deeply rooted in the fulfillment of the temple worship and the reclamation of the entire cosmos for God.
