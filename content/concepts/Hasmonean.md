---
title: "Hasmonean"
tags: [concept]
---

The Hasmonean dynasty was a ruling family of Judea and surrounding regions during classical antiquity, thriving from roughly 140 BC to 37 BC. The dynasty was established by Simon Thassi, following the successful Maccabean Revolt led by his brother Judas Maccabeus against the Seleucid Empire, which had attempted to aggressively Hellenize the Jews and desecrate the Second Temple. The Hasmoneans restored Jewish political independence and rededicated the temple (the origin of the festival of Hanukkah).

In the study of Second Temple Judaism, the Hasmonean period is critical. While initially celebrated as pious liberators, the Hasmonean rulers eventually claimed both the kingship and the high priesthood, leading to significant internal religious conflict and the rise of factions like the Pharisees, Sadducees, and Essenes. Their reign eventually succumbed to internal civil war and the intervention of the Roman Republic, leading to the installation of Herod the Great.
