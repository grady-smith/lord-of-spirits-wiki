title: "Cyrus the Great"
tags: [concept]
---
Cyrus the Great was the foundational monarch of the Achaemenid Persian Empire, remembered in history for his unprecedented policy of religious tolerance and the repatriation of displaced peoples. His conquest of Babylon in 539 BC led to the liberation of the Jewish exiles, whom he permitted to return to Jerusalem and reconstruct the Temple, ending the Babylonian Captivity.

In Orthodox theology and biblical studies, Cyrus holds the unique distinction of being the only Gentile explicitly called "Messiah" (anointed one) in the Hebrew Bible (Isaiah 45:1). This title highlights the ancient Near Eastern reality that kingship was heavily intertwined with divine appointment. By anointing Cyrus, Yahweh demonstrated His absolute supremacy over the pantheons of Babylon and Persia. It signifies a profound theological point often emphasized in the *Lord of Spirits* podcast: God can and does commandeer the rulers of the nations—who are otherwise under the dominion of fallen spiritual powers—to enact His divine will and bless His covenant people.
