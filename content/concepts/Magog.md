---
title: "Magog"
tags: [concept]
---

Magog is an enigmatic nation or geographical entity associated with "Gog" in the prophecies of Ezekiel, and later mentioned in the Book of Revelation.

In the biblical imagination, Gog and Magog represent the chaotic, antagonistic forces at the extreme edges of the known world. They symbolize the final, desperate gathering of the rebellious nations and their demonic overlords for an eschatological battle against God's people, a battle they are destined to lose.
