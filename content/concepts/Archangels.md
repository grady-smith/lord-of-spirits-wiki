---
title: "Archangels"
tags: [concept]
---
Archangels are the highest-ranking commanders among the bodiless spiritual powers that constitute the heavenly host. In the cosmology presented by the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and Eastern Orthodox tradition, the angelic realm is structured hierarchically. The Archangels serve as the chief princes of the Divine Council, directly overseeing the administration of the cosmos and executing the will of the Most High God.

Traditionally numbered as seven or eight (including Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, and Uriel, who are prominent in texts like 1 Enoch and Tobit), the Archangels lead the faithful angels in the ongoing cosmic war against the rebellious spiritual powers. They are not passive observers but active participants in human history—delivering apocalyptic revelations, guarding nations, and enforcing divine justice against the demonic entities that seek to corrupt creation.

In Orthodox liturgical life, the Archangels are continually invoked as protectors and intercessors. They represent the perfect obedience and ordered worship that humanity is called to emulate. Their existence underscores the reality that the universe is not an empty, mechanistic void, but a vibrant, populated domain governed by powerful, personal entities under the ultimate sovereignty of Christ.
