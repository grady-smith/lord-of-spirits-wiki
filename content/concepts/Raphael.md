---
title: "Raphael"
tags: [concept]
---

Raphael, whose name means "God heals," is one of the primary archangels recognized in the ancient Jewish and Orthodox Christian traditions. He features most prominently in the deuterocanonical Book of Tobit, where he disguises himself as a human to guide the young Tobias, bind the demon Asmodeus, and ultimately heal Tobit's blindness.

In the *Lord of Spirits* and broader Orthodox angelology, Raphael is a key administrator of the divine council. He is specifically associated with the healing of the earth from the corruption brought about by the fallen Watchers. According to 1 Enoch, it is Raphael who is commanded by God to bind the fallen angel Azazel and cast him into darkness, purifying the earth from the bloodshed and demonic instruction that led to the flood. He represents the divine presence actively engaged in restoration and exorcism.
