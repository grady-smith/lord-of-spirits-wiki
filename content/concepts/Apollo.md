In classical Greek mythology, Apollo was one of the most important and complex deities of the Olympian pantheon, associated with light, prophecy, music, and plague. He was the patron of the famous oracle at Delphi, where the Pythia (priestess) would deliver his prophecies.

However, in the framework of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and the ancient Christian worldview, Apollo is not merely a figment of ancient imagination or a psychological archetype. He is understood to be a very real, personal spiritual entity—a demonic being. Specifically, he is viewed as one of the rebellious members of the Divine Council who was assigned to the nations after the Tower of Babel (Deuteronomy 32) but illicitly demanded worship as a god.

When St. Paul encounters a slave girl in Philippi with a "spirit of divination" (literally a "python spirit" in the Greek text of Acts 16), he is encountering a person possessed by a subordinate demon associated with the cult of Apollo. The podcast emphasizes that the worship of Apollo involved actual communion with dark powers, and the spread of the Gospel was a direct, targeted campaign to overthrow his territorial dominion in the Greco-Roman world.
