---
title: "First Samuel"
tags: [concept]
---

First Samuel is a historical book in the Old Testament that transitions the narrative of ancient Israel from the era of the Judges to the establishment of the monarchy. It chronicles the lives of three pivotal figures: Samuel, the last judge and a great prophet; Saul, the first king of Israel whose tragic reign ends in failure; and David, the shepherd boy who is anointed as Israel's true king.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, First Samuel highlights the tension between God's direct kingship over Israel and the people's desire for a human king "like all the nations." The narrative reveals the complexities of divine providence and human agency. The anointing of David establishes the Davidic covenant, setting the stage for the ultimate messianic King, Jesus Christ, who perfectly unites the roles of priest, prophet, and king in His eternal reign.
