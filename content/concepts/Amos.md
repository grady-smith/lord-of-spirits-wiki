Amos was a prophet who operated in the 8th century BC during a time of great prosperity and simultaneous moral decay in the northern kingdom of Israel. In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Amos is a critical voice demonstrating that the proper worship of Yahweh cannot be divorced from cosmic justice and righteousness. He fiercely condemned the Israelites for maintaining the outward rituals of Temple worship while simultaneously exploiting the poor and weak.

In the ancient Near Eastern worldview, God's law is synonymous with the fundamental order of the cosmos. By oppressing the vulnerable, the Israelites were introducing chaos and aligning themselves with the rebellious spiritual powers, rendering their sacrifices not only empty, but abhorrent to God. Amos famously declared, "Let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream," emphasizing that true participation in God's kingdom requires embodying His character in the world.

Amos also pronounces divine judgments against the surrounding nations, indicating Yahweh's ultimate sovereignty over all peoples, not just Israel. His prophecies warn of the impending "Day of the Lord," which the Israelites mistakenly believed would only bring them victory, but which Amos revealed would be a day of terrible judgment against all—including Israel—who had aligned themselves with the forces of darkness and injustice.
