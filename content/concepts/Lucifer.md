---
title: "Lucifer"
tags: [concept]
---

Lucifer is a Latin term meaning "light-bringer," traditionally used to translate the Hebrew "Helel" (Day Star, Son of the Dawn) in Isaiah 14. Originally a taunt against the arrogant human king of Babylon, the language mirrors ancient myths of a lesser god attempting to usurp the high god.

In Christian tradition, this imagery became associated with the primordial rebellion of the chief fallen angel (the devil). It perfectly describes the hubris of spiritual powers attempting to ascend to the mount of assembly and usurp the worship due only to Yahweh.
