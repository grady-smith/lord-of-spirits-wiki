---
title: "El Elyon"
tags: [concept]
---
"El Elyon" is a Hebrew title often translated as "God Most High." In the ancient Near Eastern context, "El" was the generic word for a god, as well as the proper name for the chief deity of the Canaanite pantheon. The epithet "Elyon" emphasizes supreme elevation, sovereignty, and authority over all other gods. In the biblical narrative, it is notably used by Melchizedek in Genesis 14, who blesses Abraham in the name of "El Elyon, Creator of heaven and earth."

In the theological worldview of *The Lord of Spirits*, the title El Elyon is critical because it asserts Yahweh's absolute supremacy over the Divine Council and the gods of the nations. While other ancient cultures recognized high gods within their pantheons, biblical theology insists that Yahweh alone is El Elyon—the uncreated Creator who delegates authority to the heavenly host but stands infinitely above them. When the lesser gods rebelled (as described in Psalm 82), it was El Elyon who judged them, stripping them of immortality for their corruption and injustice.
