1 Enoch, also known as the Book of Enoch, is an ancient Jewish apocalyptic text that holds immense importance in the *Lord of Spirits* podcast for establishing the Second Temple Jewish worldview. Although not part of the standard canonical Hebrew Bible for most traditions (save for the Ethiopian Orthodox Church), it was highly influential for early Christians and the New Testament writers, even being directly quoted in the Epistle of Jude. 

The text is foundational for understanding the "Sin of the Watchers," an expansion on the narrative in Genesis 6. It details how a group of angelic beings (the Watchers) descended to Mount Hermon, rebelled against God, and took human wives. This illicit union produced the Nephilim (giants). Furthermore, the Watchers taught humanity forbidden knowledge, such as warfare, sorcery, and seduction, accelerating the corruption of the earth. When the Nephilim were destroyed in the Flood, their disembodied spirits became the demons that plague humanity, constantly seeking embodiment and inciting sin.

In Orthodox theology and the ancient Near Eastern context, 1 Enoch provides the necessary backdrop for understanding the cosmic war between Yahweh and the rebellious spiritual powers. Christ’s ministry, particularly his exorcisms and his transfiguration on Mount Hermon (the very site of the Watchers' rebellion), is understood directly through the lens of Enochic literature as a targeted campaign to reclaim the cosmos from these dark forces and restore humanity to its rightful place.
