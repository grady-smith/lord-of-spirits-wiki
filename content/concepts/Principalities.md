---
title: "Principalities"
tags: [concept]
---

"Principalities" (often translated from the Greek *archai*) refers to a high-ranking order of angelic beings within the unseen realm. In biblical terminology, particularly in the Epistles of St. Paul, Principalities are frequently linked with "Powers" to describe the spiritual rulers who exercise dominion over specific nations, regions, and cosmic phenomena. 

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Principalities are understood as the "sons of God" who were placed in charge of the nations following the division of humanity at the Tower of Babel (Deuteronomy 32:8). Many of these beings rebelled, choosing to rule as tyrants and accepting the illicit worship of humanity, thereby becoming the pagan gods of the ancient world. The work of Christ and His Church involves the ongoing defeat and displacement of these corrupt Principalities, liberating humanity from their subjugation and restoring the nations to the worship of the one true Creator.
