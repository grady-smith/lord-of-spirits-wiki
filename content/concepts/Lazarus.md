---
title: "Lazarus"
tags: [concept]
---

Lazarus of Bethany was a close friend of Jesus whom Christ raised from the dead after four days in the tomb. This miracle is a pivotal moment in the Gospels, serving as a direct assault on the power of death and Hades.

Furthermore, the parable of the Rich Man and Lazarus provides crucial ancient Near Eastern context for the geography of the underworld (Sheol/Hades), contrasting the comfort of "Abraham's Bosom" with the torment of separation from God, a precursor to the final judgment.
