---
title: "Adam"
tags: [concept]
---
In the theology presented by the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and Orthodox tradition, Adam is understood not merely as the biological progenitor of humanity, but as the first priest and king of creation. God placed Adam in the Garden of Eden, which functioned as the primordial temple—the cosmic mountain where heaven and earth overlapped and God held His Divine Council. Adam’s vocation was to "work and keep" the Garden, priestly language identical to the duties of the Levites in the later Tabernacle, tasked with guarding the sacred space from impurity.

Adam was also commanded to be fruitful, multiply, and subdue the earth. This meant expanding the borders of Eden, spreading the sacred space and the order of God until the entire world became a temple. However, the Fall was a failure of this priestly duty. Adam failed to guard the sanctuary against the serpent—a rebellious spiritual being—and chose to align himself with this lesser power rather than the Most High God.

Consequently, Adam and Eve were exiled from the presence of God, bringing death and corruption into the human experience. This set the stage for the necessity of the "Second Adam," Jesus Christ. Where the first Adam failed in his priestly and kingly vocation, succumbing to the dark powers, the Second Adam perfectly resisted temptation, conquered death, and successfully inaugurated the reclamation and glorification of the cosmos.
