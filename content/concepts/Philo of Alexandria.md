title: "Philo of Alexandria"
tags: [concept]
---

Philo of Alexandria, also simply known as Philo, was a deeply influential Hellenistic Jewish philosopher from the first century AD. Living in one of the most intellectually vibrant cities of the ancient Mediterranean, he sought to harmonize the theology of the Torah with Greek philosophical systems, notably utilizing the allegorical method to demonstrate that Moses was the source of the highest philosophical truths later discovered by the Greeks.

A key point of reference in the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Philo's writings reveal the diversity and theological complexity of Second Temple Judaism. He extensively developed the concept of the *Logos*, which he described as the "firstborn Son of God" and the chief of all divine intermediaries. While Philo’s interpretation was heavily imbued with Platonic idealism, his articulation of a multi-personal understanding of God's interaction with the world paved the way for early Christian apologists and theologians to articulate the dogma of the Holy Trinity and the incarnation of the Divine Word.
