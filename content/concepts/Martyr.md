---
title: "Martyr"
tags: [concept]
---

A martyr (from the Greek word for "witness") is someone who bears witness to Christ even unto death. In the early Church, martyrs were not viewed merely as tragic victims of state violence.

Instead, they were seen as victorious warriors engaged in cosmic spiritual warfare. By refusing to sacrifice to the demonic gods of the empire, and by remaining faithful through torture, martyrs actively defeated demonic powers, participating directly in Christ's victory over death.
