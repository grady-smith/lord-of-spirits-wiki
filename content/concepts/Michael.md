title: "Michael"
tags: [concept]
---

Michael is an archangel whose name means "Who is like God?" In biblical and Orthodox tradition, he is recognized as the supreme commander of the heavenly armies and the chief opponent of Satan. Within the Divine Council worldview emphasized by the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Michael holds the specific, crucial role as the patron angel and guardian of the nation of Israel (Daniel 10:21, 12:1).

In the ancient Near Eastern context, nations were understood to be governed by specific divine beings. When God divided the nations according to the "sons of God" at Babel, He later raised up Israel as His own portion. Michael is appointed as the chief prince defending Yahweh's people against the spiritual princes of other nations, such as the prince of Persia and the prince of Greece.

In Orthodox liturgical life and iconography, Michael is often depicted in military armor holding a flaming sword or a spear, actively subduing the devil. He is celebrated not just as an abstract force, but as a real, personal spiritual being who leads the faithful angels in the cosmic war against rebellious powers, a war that culminates in the casting out of the dragon in the Book of Revelation.
