The Book of Jubilees is an ancient Jewish apocryphal text deeply influential in Second Temple Judaism. It provides a retold, expanded history of the patriarchal period, divided into periods of "jubilees" (49 years).

For the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and Orthodox theology, Jubilees is crucial for its detailed expansion on the Genesis 6 narrative regarding the fall of the Watchers. It explains the origins of demons as the disembodied spirits of the dead Nephilim, which is essential for understanding the New Testament's approach to spiritual warfare.
