---
title: "Maccabees"
tags: [concept]
---

The Maccabees were a Jewish priestly family that led a successful revolt against the Seleucid Empire in the 2nd century BC, following Antiochus IV Epiphanes' desecration of the Temple.

The books of Maccabees (found in the Orthodox and Catholic Old Testament) are essential for understanding the Second Temple period. They provide crucial theological developments regarding the sanctity of martyrdom, the physical resurrection of the dead, and the practice of praying for the departed.
