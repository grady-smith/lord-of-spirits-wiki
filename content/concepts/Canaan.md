---
title: "Canaan"
tags: [concept]
---

Canaan refers to the geopolitical and cultural region in the Ancient Near East that roughly corresponds to the modern Levant (Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and parts of Syria). In biblical history, Canaan is the "Promised Land" promised to Abraham and his descendants, but it was heavily populated by various city-states practicing Canaanite religion. This religion was characterized by the worship of El, Baal, Asherah, and other deities through fertility rites, sacred prostitution, and, most notably, human (often child) sacrifice.

From the perspective of biblical cosmology and the *Lord of Spirits*, the conquest of Canaan by the Israelites under Joshua was not a mere ethnic cleansing or geopolitical expansion, but a focused campaign of cosmic holy war. The Canaanite populations, particularly those associated with the Nephilim/Anakim clans, were seen as deeply intertwined with the demonic realm. By wiping out the centers of Nephilim bloodlines and dismantling the idolatrous high places, the Israelites were executing God's judgment against the fallen divine council, systematically reclaiming Yahweh's allotted inheritance from the gods of the nations.
