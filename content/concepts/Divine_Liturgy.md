---
title: "Divine Liturgy"
tags: [concept]
---
The Divine Liturgy is the primary Eucharistic service of the Eastern Orthodox Church. It is not merely a memorial service or a didactic gathering, but a profound, mystical participation in the heavenly worship. Tracing its roots back through the early Church to the temple and synagogue worship of ancient Israel, the Liturgy is structured around the gathering of the community, the reading of the Scriptures (Liturgy of the Word), and the offering and reception of the Eucharist (Liturgy of the Faithful).

In the context of *The Lord of Spirits*, the Divine Liturgy is understood as the moment when the earthly Church ascends to join the heavenly Divine Council. The physical temple on earth becomes one with the heavenly Temple, and the congregants worship alongside the angels, archangels, and departed saints. By partaking of the Eucharist, the faithful participate in the true, unbloody sacrifice of Christ, receiving the medicine of immortality that heals the corruption of sin and binds them into the eternal life of the Holy Trinity.
