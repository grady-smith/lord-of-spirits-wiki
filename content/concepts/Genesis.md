---

Genesis is the foundational book of the Hebrew Bible and the Christian Old Testament, narrating the origins of the cosmos, humanity, and the people of Israel. It opens with the creation of the world, detailing the establishment of order out of chaos, and proceeds through the narratives of the Fall, the Flood, the Tower of Babel, and the patriarchal histories of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, Genesis is not read primarily as a modern scientific textbook, but as profound theological literature deeply in conversation with the ancient Near Eastern worldview. It establishes the reality of the unseen realm, the rebellion of spiritual beings, and the unique role of humanity as God's image-bearers in the cosmic temple. The narratives of Genesis set the stage for the entire biblical story of redemption, highlighting God's faithfulness to a fractured creation.
