---
title: "Justin Martyr"
tags: [concept]
---

Justin Martyr was a second-century Christian apologist and philosopher who rigorously defended the faith to the Roman Empire. A key element of his theology was the explicit identification of the pagan gods as demons.

He taught that the fallen angels (the gods of the nations) had deceived humanity into worshipping them through idols and mythology. This view, heavily emphasized in the *Lord of Spirits*, demonstrates that the early church viewed the Greco-Roman religious system as a localized manifestation of the cosmic rebellion.
