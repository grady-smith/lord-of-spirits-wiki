---
title: "Julius Caesar"
tags: [concept]
---

Julius Caesar was the Roman dictator whose assassination and subsequent apotheosis (being declared a god by the Roman Senate) laid the groundwork for the Roman Imperial Cult. 

From an early Christian perspective, the worship of the Emperors was not merely a civic duty but active participation in idolatry and demonic worship. Caesar's deification represents the ultimate human hubris—the attempt to ascend to the divine council by human effort—which stands in stark contrast to the self-emptying of Christ.
