Zeus is the supreme deity of the ancient Greek pantheon, the god of the sky, lightning, and thunder, who ruled from Mount Olympus. In Greek mythology, he achieved his supremacy by violently overthrowing his father, Cronus, and the Titans, establishing a chaotic and often deeply immoral rule over humanity and the other gods.

The *Lord of Spirits* podcast addresses Zeus not as a mere mythological fiction, but as a genuine, rebellious spiritual entity—likely one of the high-ranking fallen Watchers or principalities who demanded worship from the nations following the Tower of Babel. The early Christians completely understood the pagan gods to be demons. When Antiochus Epiphanes desecrated the Jerusalem Temple by setting up an altar to Zeus (the "abomination of desolation"), it was a direct territorial claim by a demonic principality against the Most High God, highlighting the intensely territorial nature of ancient spiritual warfare.
