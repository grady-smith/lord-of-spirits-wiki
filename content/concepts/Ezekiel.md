---
title: "Ezekiel"
tags: [concept]
---

Ezekiel was a major prophet of the Old Testament who ministered during the Babylonian Exile in the 6th century BC. As a priest, his prophecies are deeply concerned with the temple, holiness, and the glory of God. His visions, particularly the opening vision of the divine chariot-throne (Merkabah) and the valley of dry bones, are highly influential in both ancient Jewish mysticism and Christian theology.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* and Orthodox theology, Ezekiel provides crucial insights into the Divine Council and the spiritual realities underlying physical events. His condemnation of Israel's idolatry often describes their worship of other spiritual beings (the "gods" of the nations). His vision of the restored temple and the river of life flowing from it (Ezekiel 47) is seen as an eschatological blueprint fulfilled in Christ and the Church, representing the ultimate dwelling of God with humanity and the healing of the cosmos.
