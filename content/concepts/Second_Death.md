---
title: "Second Death"
tags: [concept]
---

The "Second Death" is an eschatological concept found primarily in the Book of Revelation, referring to the ultimate and eternal separation from God following the Final Judgment. While the "first death" is the physical death experienced by all human beings—the separation of the soul from the body—the second death is spiritual and final. In Revelation, it is described as the "lake of fire," into which Death, Hades, the beast, the false prophet, and those whose names are not found in the Book of Life are cast.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and Orthodox theology, the Second Death is understood not as a place of active torture inflicted by a vindictive God, but as the agonizing experience of God's unmediated presence by those who have entirely rejected Him. God is described as a "consuming fire"; for those who have been purified and love Him, this fire is light and warmth (theosis), but for those who are unrepentant and harbor hatred for God, that same divine presence is experienced as burning torment.

Therefore, the Second Death is the final solidification of a person's free will against God. It is the permanent state of spiritual ruin and exclusion from the life of the new creation. The Church's entire ascetic and sacramental life is oriented toward healing the soul in this life, preparing believers to endure the presence of God at the Last Judgment so that they might inherit eternal life rather than the Second Death.
