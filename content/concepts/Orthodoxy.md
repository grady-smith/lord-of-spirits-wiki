title: "Orthodoxy"
tags: [concept]
---

Orthodoxy is the state of adhering to the accepted, traditional, and true faith of the Christian Church, established by Christ, taught by the Apostles, and articulated by the Ecumenical Councils and the Church Fathers. It is the normative tradition against which all deviations (heresies) are measured.

Within the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Orthodoxy is presented not as a static set of intellectual propositions, but as the lived reality of correct relationship with God. It encompasses the entirety of the Christian life: liturgical worship, ascetic practice, moral living, and the proper understanding of the spiritual cosmos. Orthodoxy is the healing of the human condition, restoring humanity's original vocation to serve as priests and kings within the Divine Council.

To practice Orthodoxy is to engage directly in the ancient, ongoing war against the demonic forces that rebelled against God. It relies heavily on the consensus of the Fathers (*consensus patrum*) to guard against individualistic interpretations of scripture that often lead to spiritual delusion. Orthodoxy insists that the physical world is meant to be transfigured by the uncreated energies of God, a reality experienced most profoundly in the sacraments.
