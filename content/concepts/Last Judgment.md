---
title: "Last Judgment"
tags: [concept]
---

The Last Judgment is the final eschatological event where Christ, the Lord of Spirits, will return in glory to judge the living and the dead, as well as the rebellious spiritual powers.

This event is not merely a legal proceeding but the ultimate victory over chaos. The fallen angels who usurped authority over the nations will be finally deposed, and creation will be completely restored and transfigured under the rule of the Divine Council headed by Christ.
