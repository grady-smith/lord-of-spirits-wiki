title: "Prophets"
tags: [concept]
---

The Prophets (Nevi'im) constitute a major division of the Hebrew Bible, encompassing both historical narratives (Former Prophets) and the oracles of named messengers (Latter Prophets). As individuals, prophets were covenant mediators who received divine revelation directly from the heavenly assembly and were tasked with calling the nation of Israel and its leaders to repentance, warning them of impending exile and judgment.

Within the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the prophets are highlighted for their unique access to the Unseen Realm. They are described not merely as moral reformers, but as members of the Divine Council who participate in God's governance. Their apocalyptic visions, such as Ezekiel's chariot or Isaiah's seraphim, form the foundational cosmology that early Christians used to articulate the identity of Jesus Christ and the defeat of the hostile spiritual powers governing the pagan nations.
