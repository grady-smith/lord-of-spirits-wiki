---
title: "Ezra"
tags: [concept]
---

Ezra was a Jewish priest and scribe who played a pivotal role in the restoration of Jerusalem and the reconstitution of Jewish religious life after the return from the Babylonian Exile. The biblical Book of Ezra details his leadership in returning a group of exiles to Jerusalem, rebuilding the temple, and fiercely advocating for adherence to the Torah. He is traditionally credited with consolidating the Hebrew Scriptures and establishing the foundations of Second Temple Judaism.

In the context of ancient Near Eastern history, Ezra's era marks a significant transition from a localized Israelite religion centered around the pre-exilic temple to a text-based faith that could endure the diaspora. For Orthodox Christianity, Ezra's emphasis on the Word of God and proper liturgical worship foreshadows the structured life of the Church. His actions underscore the importance of maintaining covenantal faithfulness and purity in a world dominated by pagan empires.
