---
title: "Elijah"
tags: [concept]
---
Elijah the Tishbite was a major prophet in the northern kingdom of Israel during the 9th century BC. Operating during the reign of King Ahab and Queen Jezebel, Elijah's ministry was defined by intense, dramatic confrontations with the state-sponsored cult of Baal. His most famous encounter on Mount Carmel involved challenging the prophets of Baal to call down fire from heaven, demonstrating unequivocally that Yahweh, not Baal, was the true God who controlled the rain, the storm, and the fertility of the land.

In Orthodox theology and the *Lord of Spirits* perspective, Elijah is the archetype of the prophet who wages spiritual warfare against the demonic "gods of the nations." Because he was taken up bodily into heaven in a chariot of fire (a theophany of the Divine Council), Elijah is viewed as a living participant in the heavenly host. He appeared alongside Moses at the Transfiguration of Christ, representing the Prophets and witnessing to Jesus as the ultimate Lord of the living and the dead who fulfills the Law and the Prophets.
