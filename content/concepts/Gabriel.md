---
title: "Gabriel"
tags: [concept]
---

Gabriel is one of the primary archangels in Jewish, Christian, and Islamic traditions, whose name translates to "God is my strength" or "Man of God." In the biblical narrative, Gabriel functions primarily as a divine messenger, tasked with delivering crucial revelations and interpretations of visions. He appears to the prophet Daniel to explain his visions of the end times, and he announces the births of John the Baptist to Zechariah and Jesus Christ to the Virgin Mary in the Gospel of Luke.

In Orthodox theology and the worldview of the *Lord of Spirits*, Gabriel is a prominent member of the Divine Council and a key intercessor for humanity. His role is not merely communicative but deeply participatory in the unfolding of the divine plan. Along with Michael, Gabriel is venerated in the Orthodox Church as a leader of the bodiless powers of heaven, actively engaged in the spiritual warfare surrounding human salvation.
