title: "Universalism"
tags: [concept]
---

Universalism, or *apokatastasis*, is the theological belief that all intelligent creatures—including all humans and even the fallen angels or demons—will ultimately be reconciled to God and saved. While variations of this idea have existed throughout Christian history, most notably attributed to Origen of Alexandria, it was formally condemned as a heresy at the Fifth Ecumenical Council in 553 AD. The Orthodox Church firmly teaches the reality of free will and the corresponding reality of eternal separation from God for those who definitively reject Him.

The *Lord of Spirits* podcast thoroughly rejects Universalism, frequently addressing the destructive nature of absolute rebellion against God. In the context of the unseen realm, spiritual beings (demons, fallen Watchers) and humans who ally with them become fundamentally aligned with chaos and non-being. Hell is not viewed as an arbitrary torture chamber created by a vindictive God, but as the agonizing experience of God’s uncreated, fiery presence by those who hate Him. Universalism undercuts the profound gravity of human agency and the cosmic reality of the spiritual war.
