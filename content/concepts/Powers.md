---
title: "Powers"
tags: [concept]
---

In the biblical and ancient Near Eastern context, "Powers" (often grouped with Principalities, Thrones, and Dominions) refers to specific ranks of spiritual beings within the unseen realm. Found in the New Testament writings of St. Paul, this terminology describes the angelic entities—many of whom rebelled against God—who exercise authority and governance over the nations and cosmic elements.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the "Powers" are understood not as abstract social structures or psychological archetypes, but as literal, personal spiritual entities. These beings were originally appointed by Yahweh to administer the cosmos, but they fell into corruption, demanding worship and plunging the world into chaos and idolatry. A central theme of Orthodox theology and the podcast is that through His crucifixion, resurrection, and ascension, Jesus Christ has disarmed these Powers, stripping them of their authority and beginning the process of reclaiming the nations for the Most High.
