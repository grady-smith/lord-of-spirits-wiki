title: "Ecclesiastes"

---
Ecclesiastes is a philosophical and wisdom book of the Hebrew Bible, traditionally attributed to King Solomon. It is famous for its refrain, "Vanity of vanities; all is vanity" (or "hebel," meaning vapor or breath). The book rigorously examines the human condition "under the sun"—that is, life in the fallen, mortal world absent of divine intervention—and concludes that wealth, pleasure, and human wisdom are ultimately fleeting and unable to overcome the finality of death.

From an Orthodox and ancient Near Eastern perspective explored in *The Lord of Spirits*, Ecclesiastes is not a text of nihilism, but a stark, realistic assessment of a cosmos infected by sin and death. It systematically deconstructs the false promises of the fallen world and the idolatry of material pursuits. By demonstrating the ultimate futility of life isolated from the Creator, Ecclesiastes serves a pedagogical purpose, driving the reader to the conclusion that true meaning and permanence can only be found in fearing God and keeping His commandments, looking beyond the temporal world to the eternal reality of the Divine.
