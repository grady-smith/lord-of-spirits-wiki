---
title: "Micah"
tags: [concept]
---

Micah was an 8th-century BC prophet of the Kingdom of Judah whose ministry paralleled that of Isaiah. His prophecies are deeply concerned with the covenantal infidelity of both Israel and Judah, focusing intensely on the corruption of the leaders, priests, and prophets who have abandoned the justice of Yahweh for idolatry and oppression.

Within the framework of ancient Israelite religion and the *Lord of Spirits* perspective, Micah's indictments against the "high places" (such as those in Samaria and Jerusalem) are not just complaints about incorrect geography for worship, but are direct condemnations of spiritual warfare. These high places were the domains of rival gods, and the syncretistic practices occurring there invited demonic forces into the heart of God’s chosen land.

Micah is perhaps best known for his prophecy that the future ruler of Israel—the Messiah—would be born in Bethlehem Ephrathah (Micah 5:2), an entity whose "origins are from of old, from ancient times." This reinforces the Second Temple Jewish and early Christian understanding that the Messiah was a pre-existent divine figure, the Angel of the Lord, entering into human history to establish justice and conquer the false gods of the nations.
