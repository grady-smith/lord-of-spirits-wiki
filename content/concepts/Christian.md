title: "Christian"
tags: [concept]
---

The term "Christian" literally means a "follower of Christ" or "one belonging to the Anointed One," first used in Antioch to describe the disciples of Jesus (Acts 11:26). In Orthodox theology, being a Christian is not merely adhering to a set of moral guidelines or intellectual propositions; it is an ontological reality. Through Baptism and Chrismation, a person is grafted into the Body of Christ, participating directly in His divine life and taking on His royal priesthood. 

In the spiritual warfare framework of the *Lord of Spirits*, calling oneself a Christian is a declaration of cosmic allegiance. It means formally renouncing the fallen gods of the nations (demons) and swearing fealty to Yahweh, the Most High God, through His Son. Christians are seen as the reclaimed humanity, tasked with continuing Christ's mission on earth. They are called to be living temples of the Holy Spirit, pushing back the boundaries of chaos and demonic influence through asceticism, prayer, love, and participation in the sacraments, thereby bringing the nations back into God's faithful council.
