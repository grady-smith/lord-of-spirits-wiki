---
title: "Mount Hermon"
tags: [concept]
---

Mount Hermon is a prominent mountain cluster located at the northern boundary of ancient Israel. In biblical and ancient Near Eastern geography, it was heavily associated with the divine and the supernatural. Crucially, in the Second Temple Jewish tradition—particularly in the Book of 1 Enoch—Mount Hermon is identified as the exact location where the rebellious angelic beings known as the Watchers descended to earth to make a pact to corrupt humanity.

In the *Lord of Spirits* framework, Mount Hermon represents the cosmic epicenter of demonic rebellion, serving as the "anti-Zion" or the "anti-Sinai." It is strongly associated with the region of Bashan, the realm of the giant king Og and the gates of the underworld. The mountain was a site of entrenched pagan idolatry and the worship of fallen spiritual entities throughout antiquity.

This dark history makes Jesus’ actions at the base of Mount Hermon (Caesarea Philippi) profoundly significant. It is here that Christ declares the "gates of Hades" will not prevail against His Church, and it is traditionally identified as the site of the Transfiguration. By revealing His uncreated glory on or near Hermon, Christ was staging a direct, territorial assault on the heart of the demonic realm, reclaiming the high places for the Kingdom of God.
