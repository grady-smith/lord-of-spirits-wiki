---
title: "Prophet Elijah"
tags: [concept]
---

The Prophet Elijah was one of the most prominent prophets of the northern kingdom of Israel during the 9th century BC. Operating during the reign of King Ahab and Queen Jezebel, his ministry was defined by a fierce opposition to the state-sponsored worship of the Canaanite deity Baal. His legendary confrontation with the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel demonstrated Yahweh's absolute supremacy over the storm and fertility gods of the ancient Near East.

In the *Lord of Spirits* and Orthodox theology, Elijah holds significant eschatological and mystical importance. He did not experience death but was taken up into the heavens in a chariot of fire, ascending into the divine council. He appeared alongside Moses at the Transfiguration of Christ, representing the Prophets bearing witness to the uncreated light of the Son of God. His anticipated return is frequently tied to the preparation for the final Day of the Lord.
