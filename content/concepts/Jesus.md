title: "Jesus"
tags: [concept]
---
"Jesus" is the Greek transliteration (*Iesous*) of the Hebrew and Aramaic name *Yeshua* (or Joshua), which means "Yahweh saves" or "Yahweh is salvation." As the central figure of human history and the Christian faith, Jesus of Nazareth is the incarnate Word of God, the second Person of the Holy Trinity who entered human history to redeem creation.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* and biblical theology, the name Jesus is deeply significant. Just as the Old Testament Joshua led the Israelites into the Promised Land, destroying the nephilim clans and reclaiming the territory from fallen spiritual powers, Jesus is the ultimate commander who leads humanity into the eternal kingdom. He battles not against flesh and blood, but defeats the ultimate enemies: sin, death, and the demonic principalities.

Orthodox theology emphasizes that the historical man Jesus is completely identical with the pre-existent Divine Son. At His name, "every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth" (Philippians 2). To speak the name of Jesus in prayer—most notably in the Jesus Prayer ("Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner")—is to invoke the active, saving presence of God Himself, a potent weapon in spiritual warfare.
