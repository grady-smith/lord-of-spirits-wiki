---
title: "Mount Zion"
tags: [concept]
---

Mount Zion is the hill in Jerusalem upon which King David built his city, and where his son Solomon later constructed the First Temple. In biblical theology, Zion is much more than a geographic location; it is the ultimate cosmic mountain of Yahweh, superseding Mount Sinai as the permanent earthly dwelling place of the Most High God.

In the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, Mount Zion represents the decisive victory of God over the rebellious nations and the chaotic waters. It is the center of the world, the meeting place of the Divine Council, and the focal point of God's rule on earth. The Psalms repeatedly extol Zion in language that directly parallels and subverts Canaanite descriptions of Mount Zaphon, declaring that Yahweh, not Baal, is the true Lord of the cosmic mountain.

In Orthodox Christian eschatology, Zion expands beyond earthly Jerusalem. The New Testament writers and the Church Fathers identify the Church, and ultimately the New Heavens and the New Earth, as the heavenly Mount Zion. It is the destination of all faithful Christians, the place where humanity and the angelic hosts are united in unceasing worship of the Holy Trinity.
