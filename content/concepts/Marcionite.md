---
title: "Marcionite"
tags: [concept]
---

The Marcionites were followers of Marcion of Sinope, a second-century heretic who sought to completely sever Christianity from its Jewish roots. He taught that the wrathful Creator God of the Old Testament was a different, lesser entity than the loving God the Father revealed by Jesus.

The Orthodox Church aggressively combated this heresy. In the *Lord of Spirits* worldview, Marcionism is a fatal error because it destroys the continuity of Yahweh's actions; the God who led Israel out of Egypt is the exact same Logos who became incarnate as Jesus Christ.
