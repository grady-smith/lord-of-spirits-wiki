title: "Lord of Spirits"
tags: [concept]
---

The "Lord of Spirits" is a prominent divine title found in the Book of 1 Enoch. It emphasizes God's absolute sovereignty over all spiritual beings, angels, demons, and the entire unseen realm.

The podcast takes its name from this title to underscore the thoroughly enchanted, spirit-filled worldview of ancient Christianity. It serves as a reminder that the biblical narrative is fundamentally about God's cosmic victory over the rebellious powers that have enslaved humanity.
