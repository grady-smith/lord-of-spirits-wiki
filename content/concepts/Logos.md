---
title: "Logos"
tags: [concept]
---

The Logos (the Word) is the Second Person of the Holy Trinity. In the ancient Near Eastern context, the Word of Yahweh was understood as a distinct, visible manifestation of God (often identified as the Angel of the Lord) who governs the cosmos.

In Orthodox Christology, this pre-incarnate Logos, who led Israel and commanded the divine council, became flesh as Jesus Christ. The Incarnation of the Logos was the definitive invasion of earth to defeat the rebellious spiritual powers and rescue humanity.
