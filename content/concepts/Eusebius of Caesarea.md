---
title: "Eusebius of Caesarea"
tags: [concept]
---

Eusebius of Caesarea was a 4th-century Christian historian and theologian, often referred to as the "Father of Church History" for his foundational work, *Ecclesiastical History*. He was a key figure during the reign of Constantine the Great and played a prominent role in the Council of Nicaea in 325 AD. His writings preserve invaluable information about the early Church, including the succession of bishops, early Christian martyrdoms, and the development of the biblical canon.

While Eusebius's historical contributions are immense, his theological views have sometimes been scrutinized, particularly his early sympathies with Arianism before eventually signing the Nicene Creed. In discussions of ancient Christianity, Eusebius is frequently cited to understand the transition of the Church from a persecuted minority to a recognized religion within the Roman Empire, as well as the early Church's engagement with Hellenistic culture.
