---
title: "Martyrs"
tags: [concept]
---

The martyrs hold a place of profound honor in the Orthodox Church. Upon their death, they are immediately brought into the presence of God and join the divine council in the heavenly throne room.

Because they have conquered death through their faith, the martyrs serve as powerful intercessors for the Church militant on earth. Their relics are treated with immense reverence, as they carry the uncreated grace of the Holy Spirit and serve as physical touchpoints of the resurrection.
