---
title: "Mary Magdalene"
tags: [concept]
---

Mary Magdalene was a devoted disciple of Jesus Christ from whom He cast out seven demons, representing a total liberation from profound demonic oppression.

She is honored as the "apostle to the apostles" because she was the first to witness the empty tomb and the resurrected Christ. Her life represents the radical overturning of the demonic kingdom and the restoration of humanity through the power of the Lord of Spirits.
