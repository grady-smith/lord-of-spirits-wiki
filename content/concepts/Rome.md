---
title: "Rome"
tags: [concept]
---

Rome was the capital of the Roman Empire and the center of political, military, and religious authority in the ancient Mediterranean world. It was the site of the martyrdoms of the Apostles Peter and Paul and eventually became the seat of the preeminent patriarchate in the early Christian Church before the Great Schism.

In the symbolic and apocalyptic language of the New Testament and the *Lord of Spirits*, Rome is heavily identified with "Babylon." It is the epicenter of the hostile spiritual powers' earthly administration, a system sustained by violence, slavery, and the idolatrous imperial cult. The early Christian proclamation that "Jesus is Lord" was an intentionally subversive statement against the political theology of Rome, asserting that the true sovereign of the world was a Jewish man executed by Roman authorities, who had conquered the dark powers behind the empire through His resurrection.
