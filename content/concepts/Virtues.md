---
title: "Virtues"
tags: [concept]
---

In the context of Orthodox angelology, "Virtues" (sometimes translated as "Powers" or "Strongholds") denote a specific rank within the nine choirs of angels. Derived from scriptural lists of spiritual authorities (e.g., Ephesians 1:21), the Virtues are traditionally associated with the bestowal of grace, the working of miracles, and the maintenance of the physical laws of the cosmos. They govern the natural world and are imbued with an immovable, unshakeable power that reflects the divine strength.

The *Lord of Spirits* podcast discusses the Virtues in the context of the ancient understanding that the physical universe is not a dead, mechanistic clockwork, but a living reality maintained by spiritual entities. The Virtues are the angelic beings responsible for the order of nature, ensuring that creation functions according to the *logoi* (divine intentions) established by God. This starkly contrasts with the rebellious powers who seek to introduce chaos, disease, and disorder into the physical world.
