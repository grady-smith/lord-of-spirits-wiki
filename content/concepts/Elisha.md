---
Elisha was the prophetic successor to Elijah in the northern kingdom of Israel. Before Elijah’s ascension into heaven, Elisha requested a "double portion" of his master's spirit—a request rooted in the ancient Near Eastern laws of inheritance, marking Elisha as the rightful heir and leading prophet. His subsequent ministry was marked by numerous spectacular miracles, including raising the dead, multiplying food, and healing Naaman the Syrian of leprosy.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, Elisha's ministry continues the intense spiritual warfare against the demonic principalities of the surrounding nations, particularly Baal and the gods of Aram. Notably, in 2 Kings 6, Elisha demonstrates his profound awareness of the Unseen Realm when he prays for his servant's eyes to be opened, revealing that the hills are covered with horses and chariots of fire—the angelic armies of Yahweh's Divine Council—protecting them. This serves as a primary biblical text confirming the constant, invisible presence of spiritual powers operating alongside human history.
