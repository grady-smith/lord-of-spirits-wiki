title: "Epistle"
tags: [concept]
---
An epistle is a formal, didactic letter, a genre heavily utilized in the New Testament to communicate apostolic teaching, pastoral guidance, and theological correction to the early Christian communities. The Apostles (primarily Paul, Peter, James, and John) wrote these epistles not as abstract theological treatises, but as direct, pastoral responses to specific crises, liturgical questions, and cultural pressures facing the first-century Church in the Greco-Roman world.

In the context of *The Lord of Spirits*, the epistles must be read within their ancient, Second Temple Jewish matrix, rather than through the lens of modern, post-Enlightenment categories. When the epistles discuss concepts like salvation, idolatry, or the law, they are addressing congregations actively navigating a world dominated by pagan rituals and the worship of fallen spiritual beings. The epistles frequently provide the theological framework for how the victory of Christ over these demonic principalities is to be practically lived out in the liturgical and moral life of the Church.
