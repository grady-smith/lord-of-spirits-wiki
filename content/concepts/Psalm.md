---
title: "Psalm"
tags: [concept]
---

A psalm is a sacred song or hymn used in worship, particularly those found in the biblical Book of Psalms. Ranging from joyful hymns of praise to agonizing laments, the psalms formed the primary hymnal of the Second Temple period and have continued to serve as the foundational liturgical poetry for both Jewish and Christian communal worship.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, a psalm is understood not just as an expression of human emotion, but as a liturgical weapon in the cosmic struggle. The chanting of psalms in Orthodox worship actively participates in the warfare against demonic forces, continuing the ancient Israelite practice of invoking Yahweh's victory over the gods of the nations. A single psalm, such as Psalm 82, often serves as a foundational text for understanding the rebellion and judgment of the divine council.
