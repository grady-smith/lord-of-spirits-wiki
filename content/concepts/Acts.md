The Book of Acts, or the Acts of the Apostles, is St. Luke's historical account of the early Church, which the *Lord of Spirits* podcast frames as the launch of a massive, cosmic counter-offensive against the dark powers. The narrative is heavily driven by the "Deuteronomy 32 worldview," which holds that the nations of the earth were placed under the dominion of rebellious spiritual beings after the Tower of Babel. Acts documents the systematic reclamation of those nations by the Kingdom of God.

The pivotal event is Pentecost, which is understood as the exact reversal of Babel. At Babel, humanity was divided into many languages and handed over to demonic rulers; at Pentecost, the Holy Spirit enables the Apostles to preach in all languages, signaling that the time has come to gather the scattered nations back under the rule of the one true God. The disciples are empowered as the new Divine Council on earth.

As the narrative progresses, the Apostles travel throughout the Greco-Roman world, directly confronting the territorial gods of the pagan nations. Events like Paul's encounters in Ephesus, the exorcism of the slave girl with a spirit of divination, and his speech at the Areopagus in Athens are not merely cultural clashes, but spiritual warfare. Acts demonstrates the tangible defeat of the demonic principalities as communities of believers are established as outposts of Christ's kingdom within enemy territory.
