---
title: "Garden of Eden"
tags: [concept]
---

The Garden of Eden, described in the Book of Genesis, was the original dwelling place of humanity, a paradise created by God where Adam and Eve lived in direct communion with Him. In the context of the ancient Near East, Eden is understood not merely as a beautiful park but as a cosmic temple, the meeting place of heaven and earth where God's presence uniquely resided. Rivers flowed from Eden to water the earth, a common motif for the source of life and divine blessing in ancient cosmologies.

In Orthodox theology and the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Eden represents humanity's original vocation to serve as priests of creation, tasked with expanding the borders of this divine sanctuary to encompass the whole world. The expulsion from Eden following the Fall was a tragic separation from the Tree of Life. However, the eschatological vision of the New Jerusalem in Revelation is depicted as a restored and glorified Eden, a massive city-temple where God dwells fully with His people once again.
