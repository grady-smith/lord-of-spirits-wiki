---
title: "Divine Council"
tags: [concept]
---
The Divine Council is a prominent concept in the ancient Near East and the Hebrew Bible, referring to the heavenly assembly of spiritual beings (angels, "sons of God," or *elohim*) who surround Yahweh, deliberate with Him, and execute His will throughout the cosmos. Psalm 82 is a classic text portraying God standing in the "divine council" to pass judgment on its corrupt members.

In *The Lord of Spirits* podcast and Orthodox theology, the Divine Council is central to understanding cosmic geography and spiritual warfare. At the Tower of Babel, God delegated the administration of the Gentile nations to members of this council (Deuteronomy 32:8-9). However, many of these beings rebelled, demanding worship from humans and ruling with injustice—becoming the pagan gods of antiquity. A major theme of the New Testament is Christ’s defeat of these rebellious council members (the "principalities and powers") and the seating of glorified human saints (like the Apostles) in their vacated cosmic thrones, thus reconstituting the Divine Council with humanity.
