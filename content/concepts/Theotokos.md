---
title: "Theotokos"
tags: [concept]
---

Theotokos is a Greek title meaning "God-bearer" or "Mother of God," officially ascribed to the Virgin Mary at the Council of Ephesus in 431 AD. The title was primarily a Christological assertion against Nestorianism; affirming that Mary gave birth to God is necessary to protect the truth that Jesus Christ is a single, undivided divine person who assumed human nature from the moment of conception. To deny the title Theotokos is to imply that Mary merely gave birth to a human person who was later conjoined with the divine Logos.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, the Theotokos holds a monumental role in the cosmic architecture of salvation. She is viewed as the ultimate fulfillment of the Ark of the Covenant, physically containing the uncontainable God. Furthermore, in the divine council worldview, she assumes the role of the Queen Mother (Gebirah) in the Kingdom of Heaven, seated at the right hand of her Son, the King. As the chief of all saints, she leads the triumphant heavenly host, actively participating in the spiritual warfare against the demonic realm through her intercession.
