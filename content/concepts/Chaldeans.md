---
title: "Chaldeans"
tags: [concept]
---

The Chaldeans were a Semitic people who lived in southern Babylonia, eventually rising to prominence and establishing the Neo-Babylonian Empire in the late 7th century BC. Under Chaldean kings like Nabopolassar and his son Nebuchadnezzar II, Babylon conquered the Assyrian Empire and subsequently destroyed the Kingdom of Judah, leading to the Babylonian Exile. In biblical literature, particularly in the Book of Daniel, the term "Chaldeans" also became synonymous with the elite class of astrologers, magicians, and wise men attached to the Babylonian court.

In the context of biblical cosmology, the Chaldeans represent the pinnacle of demonically inspired human wisdom and imperial power. Their renowned expertise in astrology and divination was not seen as mere superstition, but as an active engagement with the fallen spiritual powers (the hosts of heaven) who govern the nations. When the Prophet Daniel and his companions surpass the Chaldeans in wisdom, it is a demonstration of the superiority of the Holy Spirit and the Most High God over the rebellious divine council and the dark arts they imparted to humanity.
