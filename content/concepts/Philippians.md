title: "Philippians"
tags: [concept]
---

Philippians is an epistle in the New Testament written by the Apostle Paul to the Christian community in Philippi, a major Roman colony in Macedonia. Written while Paul was imprisoned, the letter is characterized by its themes of joy, humility, and partnership in the Gospel, despite suffering. 

Theologically, within the framework discussed in the *Lord of Spirits*, the Epistle to the Philippians contains one of the most profound Christological texts in early Christianity: the "Christ Hymn" of chapter 2. This passage outlines the incarnation of Christ, his voluntary self-emptying (kenosis), his obedience unto death, and his ultimate exaltation. This exaltation concludes with every knee bowing in heaven, on earth, and under the earth, illustrating Yahweh’s cosmic victory over all spiritual powers and principalities, a key theme in the reclamation of the cosmos from fallen divine beings.
