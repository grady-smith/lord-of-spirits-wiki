---
title: "Matins"
tags: [concept]
---

Matins (often called Orthros in the Greek tradition) is the primary morning prayer service in the Orthodox Church. It serves to prepare the faithful for the Eucharistic gathering of the Divine Liturgy.

The service is heavily theological, involving a daily recounting of salvation history through the Psalms, hymns, and the reading of the Gospel. It is a way for the earthly congregation to join in the continuous, unceasing praise of the angelic hosts at the beginning of each day.
