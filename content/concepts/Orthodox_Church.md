---
title: "Orthodox Church"
tags: [concept]
---

The Orthodox Church (or Eastern Orthodox Church) is the communion of autocephalous Christian churches that claim unbroken historical, theological, and apostolic continuity with the early Church founded by Jesus Christ and His Apostles. It is characterized by its adherence to the first seven Ecumenical Councils, its rich liturgical and sacramental life, and its preservation of ancient Christian theology.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, the Orthodox Church is understood as the true continuation of the Israel of the Old Testament. It is the visible manifestation of the Kingdom of God on earth, functioning as the localized presence of the Divine Council. The Church's sacraments, particularly the Eucharist, are the means by which humanity is united to Christ, defeating the forces of death and demonic corruption.

The Orthodox Church maintains an "enchanted" worldview, vehemently resisting the rationalistic and materialistic reductions of modernism. It treats the spiritual realm, angels, demons, and the active presence of God in physical matter as immediate, daily realities. The Church's life—its fasting, feasts, iconography, and prayers—is fundamentally structured as an ongoing cosmic war against the fallen powers, bringing healing and transfiguration to the world.
