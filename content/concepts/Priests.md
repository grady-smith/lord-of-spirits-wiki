---
title: "Priests"
tags: [concept]
---

Priests were the specific individuals consecrated for the service of the altar and the mediation between God and the people. In ancient Israel, they were tasked with performing sacrifices, examining ritual purity, interpreting the law, and blessing the congregation. They stood on the boundary between the sacred and the profane, engaging in rigorous purification to safely approach the holy presence of Yahweh.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, priests are understood as essential functionaries in the cosmic battle against corruption and death. Their sacrifices were not designed to appease an angry God, but to cleanse the sanctuary from the pollution of human sin, making it a hospitable dwelling for the divine presence. In the Church, while all believers participate in a "royal priesthood" through prayer and offering their lives, ordained priests specifically preside over the Eucharistic assembly, continuing the ancient liturgical pattern of joining earthly worship to the heavenly Divine Council.
