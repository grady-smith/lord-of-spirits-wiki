title: "Book of Enoch"
tags: [concept]

The *Book of Enoch* (specifically 1 Enoch) is an ancient Jewish apocalyptic text attributed to the biblical figure Enoch, the great-grandfather of Noah. Although not included in the canonical scriptures of most Christian traditions (with the notable exception of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church), 1 Enoch was wildly popular in Second Temple Judaism and deeply influential on the writers of the New Testament. The text is most famous for its detailed expansion of the Genesis 6 narrative, describing how a group of angels called the "Watchers" rebelled against God, descended to Mount Hermon, mated with human women to produce the monstrous Nephilim, and taught forbidden knowledge to humanity.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and Orthodox theology, the *Book of Enoch* is treated as an indispensable key for understanding the worldview of the biblical authors. It provides the essential backstory for the proliferation of demons (understood as the disembodied spirits of the dead Nephilim) and the origin of human idolatry and warfare. While not considered inspired scripture by the Eastern Orthodox Church, the theology, cosmology, and spiritual history contained in the *Book of Enoch* are seen as accurately reflecting the ancient, apostolic understanding of the unseen realm and Christ's ultimate victory over the fallen powers.
