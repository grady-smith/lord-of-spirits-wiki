---
title: "David"
tags: [concept]
---
David was the second and greatest king of the united Kingdom of Israel, a shepherd-turned-warrior from the tribe of Judah. In ancient Israelite history, he is renowned for capturing Jerusalem, establishing it as the political and religious center, and bringing the Ark of the Covenant into the city. He is the traditional author of many of the Psalms, which form the core of liturgical worship in both the Second Temple and the Orthodox Church.

Theologically, the Davidic Covenant (2 Samuel 7) is a cornerstone of biblical messianism. God promised that a descendant of David would sit upon his throne eternally. In the *Lord of Spirits* perspective, David functions as the archetypal anointed king who acts as Yahweh's vice-regent on earth, battling the chaos monsters (such as the giant Goliath, tying back to the Nephilim traditions) and hostile nations animated by fallen gods. Jesus Christ, the ultimate "Son of David," fulfills this typological role by inaugurating an eternal, cosmic kingdom and achieving final victory over the spiritual forces of darkness.
