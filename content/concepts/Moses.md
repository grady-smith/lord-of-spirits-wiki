---
title: "Moses"
tags: [concept]
---

Moses is the foundational prophet and lawgiver of Israel, chosen by Yahweh to lead the Israelites out of Egyptian bondage. In the context of ancient Near Eastern theology and the *Lord of Spirits* framework, Moses' role goes far beyond merely transmitting a legal code; he is a primary participant in the Divine Council. He encounters God face-to-face, speaking with the Angel of the Lord (the pre-incarnate Christ) in the burning bush and on Mount Sinai.

The Exodus narrative, led by Moses, is a cosmic conflict. The ten plagues are direct judgments against the gods of Egypt, demonstrating Yahweh's supreme authority over the spiritual powers of the nations. Moses acts as God’s vice-regent, wielding divine authority to defeat the demonic forces of chaos and death represented by Pharaoh and the Egyptian pantheon.

Furthermore, Moses is presented as the architect of the Tabernacle, an earthly replica of God's heavenly throne room which Moses directly witnessed during his ascent of Mount Sinai. By establishing the priesthood and the liturgical life of Israel, Moses localized the presence of God among the people, creating a safe zone of holiness in a world dominated by hostile spiritual entities.
