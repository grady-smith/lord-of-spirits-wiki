Martin Luther was the seminal figure of the 16th-century Protestant Reformation. While intending to reform the Western Church, his theological shifts had massive, long-term implications for how Western Christians view the cosmos.

In the context of the podcast, Luther's removal of the Deuterocanonical books (the Apocrypha) and his shift toward a more individualized, forensic soteriology (salvation as a legal transaction) are often seen as contributing factors to the modern disenchantment of the Western worldview, moving away from the ancient understanding of spiritual warfare.
