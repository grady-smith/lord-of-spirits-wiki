St. Paisios of Mount Athos (1924–1994) is a highly venerated modern Orthodox saint, renowned for his profound asceticism, spiritual counsel, and deep love for all creation. Spending much of his life as a monk on Mount Athos, he became a spiritual father to thousands of pilgrims seeking guidance in an increasingly secularized world. His teachings are characterized by simplicity, profound spiritual insight, and an emphasis on "philotimo"—a loving, joyful, and grateful responsiveness to God's love.

From the perspective of spiritual warfare and the unseen realm, St. Paisios's life and counsels are frequently referenced to illustrate the ongoing, tangible reality of demonic attacks and angelic assistance in the modern era. He often spoke plainly about his encounters with the demonic and the protection of the saints and the Virgin Mary, reminding believers that the cosmic conflict described in ancient texts remains an active reality. His deep spiritual sensitivity reinforces the Orthodox understanding that the unseen realm profoundly overlaps with our everyday lives.
