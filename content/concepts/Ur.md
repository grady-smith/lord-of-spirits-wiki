---

Ur was a prominent and incredibly ancient Sumerian city-state located in Mesopotamia (modern-day Iraq). It was a major center of civilization, trade, and pagan religion in the ancient Near East, dominated by the great ziggurat dedicated to Nanna (Sin), the moon god. In the Biblical narrative, Ur of the Chaldeans is famous as the original home of the patriarch Abraham.

In the *Lord of Spirits* context, Ur represents the epicenter of post-Babel paganism and the domination of the fallen divine beings. When Yahweh calls Abraham out of Ur (Genesis 12), it is a radical act of spiritual extraction. God is pulling Abraham out of a society thoroughly controlled by the rebellious principalities to begin a new, holy nation. Abraham's departure from the sophisticated, idolatrous culture of Ur marks the beginning of God's long campaign to reclaim the nations from the dark powers to which they were surrendered at the Tower of Babel.
