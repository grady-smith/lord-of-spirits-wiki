title: "Watchers"
tags: [concept]
---

The Watchers are a class of spiritual beings prominent in Second Temple Jewish literature, particularly the Book of Enoch, and referenced in the canonical books of Genesis (the "sons of God" in Gen 6), Daniel, and the epistles of Peter and Jude. In their unfallen state, they were angelic beings tasked with overseeing humanity. However, a faction of these Watchers rebelled, descended to Mount Hermon, mated with human women (producing the Nephilim), and illicitly taught humanity the destructive arts of war, sorcery, and idolatry.

The narrative of the fallen Watchers is a cornerstone of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast. The hosts argue that the sin of the Watchers is equally as important as the Fall of Adam and Eve for understanding the proliferation of evil and demonic activity in the world. The early Church universally understood the gods of the pagan nations to be these fallen Watchers or their demonic offspring. Christ’s ministry, particularly his actions around Mount Hermon (Caesarea Philippi), is portrayed as a direct assault on the stronghold of the Watchers, signaling their ultimate defeat.
