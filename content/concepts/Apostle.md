The word "Apostle" derives from the Greek *apostolos*, meaning "one who is sent." In the ancient world, an apostle was not just a messenger, but a plenipotentiary—a representative authorized to speak and act with the full authority of the one who sent them. In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Apostles are fundamentally understood as the earthly counterpart to God's heavenly Divine Council.

When Christ chose the Twelve Apostles, He was symbolically reconstituting the twelve tribes of Israel and establishing the foundation for a new, cosmic administration. Just as the nations were divided according to the number of the "sons of God" (the rebellious angels) at the Tower of Babel, Christ sends out His Apostles—often numbered at 70 or 72 in the broader group—to systematically reclaim those exactly 70 nations back for the Kingdom of God.

Therefore, the work of an Apostle is intrinsically an act of spiritual warfare. When an Apostle entered a pagan city to preach the Gospel, perform exorcisms, and establish a local church, they were acting as Christ's commanding generals, directly invading the territory of the demonic gods, dismantling their power structures, and establishing an outpost of Eden.
