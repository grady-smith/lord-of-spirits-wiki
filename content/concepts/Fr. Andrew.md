Father Andrew Stephen Damick is an Orthodox Christian priest, author, and podcaster, best known for co-hosting the *Lord of Spirits* podcast alongside Father Stephen De Young. A clergyman in the Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese of North America, he has dedicated much of his ministry to public theology, exploring the intersection of ancient Christian faith, history, and modern culture.

In the *Lord of Spirits*, Father Andrew often serves as the conversational bridge, guiding discussions that unpack complex ancient Near Eastern contexts and Second Temple Jewish theology into accessible Orthodox Christian teaching. His work emphasizes the reality of the unseen realm, the spiritual warfare inherent in the Christian life, and the deep, mystical continuity between the ancient biblical worldview and the ongoing liturgical life of the Orthodox Church.
