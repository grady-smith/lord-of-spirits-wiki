---
title: "Mark"
tags: [concept]
---

The Gospel of Mark presents Jesus as a divine warrior taking immediate and aggressive action to invade the territory of the rebellious spiritual powers. It is characterized by the repeated use of the word "immediately" (euthys).

Mark heavily features exorcisms and confrontations with demonic forces. It paints a picture of a world held captive by evil spirits, with Christ arriving as the stronger man to bind the strong man (the devil) and plunder his house (humanity).
