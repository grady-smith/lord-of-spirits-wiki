---
title: "Judah"
tags: [concept]
---

Judah is the tribe of Israel from which the Davidic dynasty and ultimately the Messiah arose. Following the split of the unified monarchy, it became the southern kingdom, centered around Jerusalem and the Temple.

The Kingdom of Judah was tasked with maintaining the earthly throne of Yahweh and the cosmic center of worship. Despite its eventual fall into idolatry and exile, it remained the designated vehicle through which the Lord of Spirits would incarnate to defeat the rebellious gods of the nations.
