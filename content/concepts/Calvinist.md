---
title: "Calvinist"
tags: [concept]
---

A Calvinist is an adherent of the theological system derived from the teachings of John Calvin and the Reformed tradition. Calvinists structure their understanding of salvation, the church, and the cosmos around the "Doctrines of Grace" (often summarized by the acronym TULIP: Total Depravity, Unconditional Election, Limited Atonement, Irresistible Grace, and Perseverance of the Saints). For a Calvinist, the primary lens through which scripture is interpreted is the absolute, decreeing sovereignty of God over every specific event in human history.

In comparative theological discussions, particularly on the *Lord of Spirits*, the Calvinist perspective serves as a profound contrast to the Orthodox worldview. Where a Calvinist might see God's exhaustive meticulous control over every detail, Orthodoxy emphasizes a cosmos populated by free, rational agents (both human and angelic) whose rebellion introduces genuine chaos, which God actively manages and redeems. Furthermore, the Calvinist view of the sacraments as primarily symbolic or cognitive "seals" contrasts sharply with the Orthodox understanding of them as physical, transfiguring encounters with the uncreated energies of God that objectively cleanse the cosmos.
