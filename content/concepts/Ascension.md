---
title: "Ascension"
tags: [concept]
---

In Orthodox theology and biblical cosmology, the Ascension of Christ is not merely a departure into the sky, but a cosmic enthronement. It marks the moment when the incarnate Son of God, having defeated death and Hades, ascends to take His seat at the right hand of the Father. This act places humanity, now united with the divine in the person of Christ, within the Divine Council at the highest level of heaven, effectively replacing the fallen spiritual powers that previously held dominion over the nations. 

From an Ancient Near Eastern perspective, the Ascension parallels and subverts the ancient enthronement motifs of storm gods (like Baal or Marduk) ascending to their temples after defeating chaos monsters. In the biblical narrative, Christ's ascent is the ultimate fulfillment of these echoes; He rides the "chariot of the clouds" (a well-known divine attribute in the ANE) to claim true and eternal sovereignty over all spiritual and earthly realms. Through the Ascension, Christ fills all things and bridges the gap between heaven and earth, inaugurating the Kingdom of God and opening the path for human theosis.
