---
title: "Ancient Israel"
tags: [concept]
---
Ancient Israel was the nation uniquely called by Yahweh to be His own inheritance in the aftermath of the Tower of Babel. According to the "Deuteronomy 32 worldview" frequently discussed in the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, when God disinherited the nations of the earth and placed them under the administration of lesser spiritual beings (who subsequently rebelled and became the pagan gods), He simultaneously chose Abraham to birth a new nation. Israel was to be the singular beachhead of the Most High God in a rebel-occupied world.

The vocation of Ancient Israel was essentially priestly and cosmic. They were called to be a "kingdom of priests and a holy nation," meaning they were to mediate the presence of God to the rest of the world. The Tabernacle, and later the Temple in Jerusalem, functioned as a restored Eden—the localized intersection of heaven and earth. By maintaining ritual purity and offering correct sacrifices, Israel pushed back the chaotic and demonic forces surrounding them.

However, the history of Ancient Israel is largely a tragic narrative of failing to uphold this vocation. Seduced by the gods of the surrounding nations (Baal, Asherah, etc.), Israel frequently fell into idolatry, breaking the covenant and aligning with the dark powers. This persistent failure ultimately necessitated the coming of the Messiah, Jesus Christ, who fulfilled the destiny of Israel, defeated the fallen gods, and expanded the covenant to include all nations through the Church.
