---
title: "Gog"
tags: [concept]
---

Gog is a mysterious apocalyptic figure introduced in the Book of Ezekiel (chapters 38-39), described as the ruler of the land of Magog and a chief prince of nations from the far north. In Ezekiel's prophecy, Gog leads a massive, terrifying coalition of nations against the restored people of Israel, only to be dramatically and utterly destroyed by the direct intervention of God.

In Second Temple Judaism and early Christian theology, Gog (often paired with Magog) became symbolic of the ultimate cosmic forces of chaos and rebellion that will wage the final battle against God's people at the end of the age. In the Book of Revelation (chapter 20), Gog and Magog represent the nations deceived by Satan who surround the "camp of the saints" before being consumed by divine fire. They embody the final, futile surge of evil before the establishment of the New Jerusalem.
