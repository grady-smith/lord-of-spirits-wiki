The Acts of the Apostles, authored by St. Luke, serves as the historical continuation of the Gospel narrative, depicting the early Church's explosive growth. In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, it is read as the tactical history of Christ's campaign to reclaim the cosmos from the fallen spiritual powers. Following Christ's ascension and enthronement, Acts illustrates the execution of His divine authority on earth through His Apostles, who act as His emissaries to the nations.

A central theme in Acts is the reversal of the disinheritance of the nations that occurred at the Tower of Babel (Deuteronomy 32). The descent of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost, accompanied by fire and miraculous translation of languages, is a direct signal that Yahweh is calling all peoples back to Himself. The Apostles are sent out to systematically dismantle the geographic and spiritual strongholds of the rebellious gods that had ruled the gentile world for millennia.

The book is filled with accounts of spiritual warfare—exorcisms, the burning of magic books, and direct confrontations with pagan cults, such as the worship of Artemis in Ephesus. These are not merely cultural disagreements, but manifestations of the cosmic war. The establishment of local churches throughout the Roman Empire marks the planting of new "Edens," sacred spaces where the true God is worshipped and the power of the demonic principalities is broken.
