title: "Bartholomew"
tags: [concept]
---

Bartholomew, often identified with Nathanael from the Gospel of John, is one of the Twelve Apostles of Jesus Christ. His name is an Aramaic patronymic meaning "Son of Tolmai." According to early Christian tradition, after the Ascension of Christ and the descent of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost, Bartholomew traveled extensively as a missionary, bringing the Gospel to regions as far as India and Greater Armenia, where he is credited alongside the Apostle Thaddeus with establishing the Armenian Apostolic Church.

In the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, the sending of the Apostles is understood as the deployment of Christ's newly established Divine Council to reclaim the earth from the demonic principalities. The martyrdom of Bartholomew—traditionally believed to have been flayed alive and beheaded in Armenia—illustrates the intense spiritual warfare inherent in this mission. The fallen gods of the nations fiercely violently opposed the preaching of the Gospel, yet through the martyrdom of saints like Bartholomew, the Church paradoxically triumphed, consecrating the earth and disarming the demonic powers through faithful witness unto death.
