title: "Virgin Mary"
tags: [concept]
---

The Virgin Mary, revered as the Theotokos (God-bearer) in the Orthodox Church, is the mother of Jesus Christ. Her pivotal "Yes" (Fiat) at the Annunciation allowed for the Incarnation of the Son of God, reversing the disobedience of Eve. In Orthodox theology, she is honored above all created beings—"more honorable than the Cherubim and beyond compare more glorious than the Seraphim"—due to her unique role in the salvation of the cosmos.

Within the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, the Virgin Mary is fundamentally understood through the lens of the Queen Mother (Gebirah) of the Davidic Kingdom. Because Christ is the ultimate Davidic King, His mother reigns as Queen. In the spiritual warfare of the unseen realm, she is the supreme intercessor and the leader of the heavenly host against the demonic powers. She is the ultimate, perfected example of theosis, demonstrating humanity's intended glory within the Divine Council.
