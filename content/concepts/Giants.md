---
title: "Giants"
tags: [concept]
---

In the biblical and ancient Near Eastern context, "Giants" (often identified with the Nephilim in Genesis 6) are understood not merely as unusually tall humans, but as the hybrid offspring of rebellious divine beings (the Watchers) and human women. These figures, such as Og of Bashan and Goliath, are depicted as tyrannical warriors and the source of immense corruption and violence on the earth prior to the Flood and during the conquest of Canaan.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Giants represent a critical part of the cosmic rebellion against God. They embody the unlawful mixing of the heavenly and earthly realms and the introduction of forbidden knowledge (such as metallurgy and warfare) to humanity. The spirits of these deceased giants are identified in Second Temple literature, such as 1 Enoch, as the origin of demons—disembodied evil spirits that continue to afflict humanity and oppose God's purposes.
