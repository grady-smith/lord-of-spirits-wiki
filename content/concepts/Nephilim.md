---
title: "Nephilim"
tags: [concept]
---

The Nephilim are the giant hybrid offspring resulting from the illicit union between the "sons of God" (rebellious angelic beings known as Watchers) and the "daughters of men" detailed in Genesis 6. In ancient Near Eastern context and Second Temple Jewish literature (like 1 Enoch), the Nephilim were understood as tyrannical, violent beings who consumed the earth's resources and corrupted human society with bloodshed and dark arts.

In the *Lord of Spirits* framework, the existence of the Nephilim is central to understanding the spiritual warfare of the Old Testament. The global flood of Noah was brought about primarily to cleanse the earth of the Nephilim and the cosmic corruption they caused. When the Nephilim physically died, their unnatural hybrid spirits became the disembodied unclean spirits (demons) that continue to plague humanity in the New Testament and the present age.

Following the flood, the bloodlines or clans of the giants reappeared (e.g., the Anakim, Rephaim), concentrated in areas like Canaan and Bashan. The conquest of Canaan by Joshua was not a standard ethnic war, but a targeted campaign of cosmic warfare intended to wipe out the remaining giant clans and reclaim the land for Yahweh, clearing out the remnants of demonic hybridity.
