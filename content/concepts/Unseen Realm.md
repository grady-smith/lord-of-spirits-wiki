---
title: "Unseen Realm"
tags: [concept]
---

The "Unseen Realm" refers to the spiritual dimension of reality that exists alongside, and constantly interacts with, the material world. In the ancient Near Eastern and Biblical worldview, this realm is populated by a vast hierarchy of spiritual beings—angels, demons, Watchers, archangels, and the Divine Council. The unseen realm is not geographically distant or abstract; it is intimately overlaid onto the physical cosmos, influencing and participating in human affairs.

This concept is the central thesis of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast. The hosts argue that modern Western Christianity has largely adopted a secularized, materialistic worldview that ignores or demythologizes the unseen realm, reducing angels and demons to metaphors. Reclaiming the Orthodox and ancient Biblical perspective requires understanding that history and daily life are heavily contested spaces where invisible spiritual powers are actively engaged in conflict, and that Christian liturgical and sacramental life is the primary means of participating in this cosmic war.
