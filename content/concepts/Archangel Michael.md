---
title: "Archangel Michael"
tags: [concept]
---
The Archangel Michael, whose name is a battle-cry meaning "Who is like God?", is the chief commander of the heavenly armies of the Most High God. In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Michael is central to understanding the ongoing cosmic war in the unseen realm. In the Book of Daniel, he is explicitly identified as the "great prince" who stands guard over the people of Israel, operating as their spiritual patron and defender against the demonic princes of the gentile nations, such as the Prince of Persia.

Orthodox theology and Second Temple Jewish literature frequently depict Michael leading the faithful angelic host in direct combat against Satan and the rebellious angels. In the Book of Revelation, it is Michael and his angels who fight against the Dragon, casting him down from the heavenly places. This represents the enforcing of Christ's victory achieved on the cross.

As the commander of the bodiless powers, Michael is not just a protector but an active agent in the destruction of idolatry and demonic strongholds. In Orthodox iconography, he is almost always depicted in military armor holding a flaming sword, a physical representation of the ferocious, ongoing spiritual warfare required to guard the sanctity of creation and execute God's judgments against the forces of chaos.
