---
title: "Prophecy"
tags: [concept]
---

Prophecy, in the biblical and ancient Near Eastern context, is the communication of divine will and revelation to humanity through a chosen intermediary. While modern definitions often reduce prophecy to mere fortune-telling or predicting the future, ancient prophecy was primarily concerned with speaking truth to power, calling for repentance, and interpreting the current spiritual reality in light of God's covenantal demands.

Within the *Lord of Spirits* framework, prophecy is deeply tied to the Divine Council. True prophets, such as Isaiah or Jeremiah, were individuals who were granted access to the heavenly assembly—either through visions or direct encounters—and were then commissioned to deliver the council's decrees to earth. Prophecy is therefore an administrative act of the heavenly court, bringing the ultimate reality of the unseen realm to bear on the immediate, material circumstances of God's people.
