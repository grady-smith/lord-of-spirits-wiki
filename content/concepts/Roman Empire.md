---
title: "Roman Empire"
tags: [concept]
---

The Roman Empire was the dominant political and military power of the Mediterranean world during the time of Christ and the early Church. It was characterized by an aggressive military expansion, a complex system of patronage, and the widespread practice of the imperial cult, which mandated the worship of the emperor as a divine figure and the integration of conquered peoples' gods into the Roman pantheon.

In the *Lord of Spirits* and biblical apocalyptic literature, the Roman Empire is frequently depicted as the ultimate manifestation of the demonic beast from the sea. It represents the hostile spiritual powers working through human political structures to demand idolatrous worship and persecute the faithful. Early Christians refused to participate in the imperial cult, recognizing that to offer incense to the emperor was to sacrifice to demons. The conversion of the empire under Constantine is seen as a massive victory in the cosmic war, breaking the public power of pagan deities over the state.
