The Eastern Orthodox Church is the second-largest Christian communion in the world, tracing its historical, theological, and liturgical continuity directly back to the Apostles and the early Christian communities of the eastern Mediterranean and Middle East. It is characterized by its adherence to the seven Ecumenical Councils, its rich liturgical life, and its profound emphasis on mystical theology and theosis (the process of becoming united with God).

In the context of *The Lord of Spirits* podcast, Eastern Orthodoxy is presented not merely as a set of dogmatic propositions, but as the faithful continuation of the ancient, enchanted worldview of Second Temple Judaism. Orthodox theology heavily emphasizes the reality of the Unseen Realm, the ongoing cosmic war against fallen spiritual powers, and the deeply sacramental nature of creation. Unlike modern, post-Enlightenment expressions of Christianity that often demythologize the faith, Eastern Orthodoxy preserves the ancient understanding that humans live in a cosmos densely populated by angels, demons, and the ever-present, transfiguring uncreated energies of the Holy Trinity.
