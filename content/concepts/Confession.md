title: "Confession"

---

Confession, or the Sacrament of Repentance, is the Orthodox practice of acknowledging one's sins before God in the presence of a priest, seeking forgiveness and spiritual healing. It is not viewed primarily as a juridical tribunal where a guilty verdict is commuted, but rather as a hospital for the soul. The priest acts not as a judge, but as a witness and a spiritual physician, facilitating the restoration of the penitent's relationship with God and the community of the Church.

In the spiritual warfare paradigm of the *Lord of Spirits*, Confession is a vital tactical practice. Sin is understood as a willing participation in the chaos and corruption of the fallen spiritual powers. By keeping sins hidden, a person grants a foothold to the demonic realm. Confession is an act of exposure and renunciation; bringing one's sins into the light severs the legal and spiritual claims the enemy has upon the individual. It is an ongoing renewal of the baptismal vow, realigning the believer with the faithful Divine Council and washing away the defilement that accrues from living in a fallen world.
