---
title: "Judges"
tags: [concept]
---

The period of the Judges in Israelite history was characterized by a cycle of apostasy, oppression, and deliverance. Charismatic leaders, empowered by the Spirit of God, were raised up to deliver Israel from surrounding nations.

These conflicts were ultimately proxy wars between Yahweh and the gods of the Canaanites. The Judges were tasked not only with military deliverance but with combating the deep-seated spiritual adultery of the Israelites, who constantly fell back into the worship of regional demonic powers.
