---
title: "Paradise"
tags: [concept]
---

Paradise originates from an ancient Persian word meaning a walled garden or royal enclosure. In biblical theology, it is primarily associated with the Garden of Eden, the original sanctuary where God dwelt with humanity. However, Paradise is not merely a lost historical location; it represents the ultimate state of communion between God and creation.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Paradise is understood as the cosmic mountain and the true Holy of Holies. It is the place where heaven and earth overlap. When humanity was expelled from Eden, they were exiled from the direct presence of the Divine Council. Throughout the Old Testament, the Tabernacle and the Temple were constructed as architectural replicas of Paradise, designed to bring a localized portion of Eden back to earth.

In Orthodox eschatology, Paradise is the destination of the righteous after death (as Christ promised the thief on the cross: "Today you will be with me in Paradise") and the ultimate reality of the New Heavens and the New Earth. The Tree of Life, once guarded by the Cherubim, is fully accessible again in Christ, particularly through the sacrament of the Eucharist, which allows believers to partake of Paradise here and now.
