---
title: "Apostles"
tags: [concept]
---
The Apostles are the men chosen by Jesus Christ to act as His plenipotentiary representatives on earth. In the worldview presented by the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, their appointment is an event of massive cosmic significance. The Twelve Apostles correspond directly to the twelve tribes of Israel, representing the restoration of the nation around its true King. They also symbolically correspond to the ancient understanding of cosmic order, such as the twelve constellations or signs of the Zodiac, indicating that Christ is restoring the fundamental order of the universe.

Following the descent of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost, the Apostles function as the restored, earthly Divine Council. Their mission is driven by the mandate to reverse the events of the Tower of Babel. The nations of the earth had been enslaved by the rebellious "gods of the nations," but the Apostles are sent out into the Gentile world to preach the Gospel, perform exorcisms, and reclaim humanity for the Most High God.

Through the establishment of local churches, the ordination of bishops, and the celebration of the Eucharist, the Apostles systematically planted outposts of the Kingdom of God within enemy territory. Their ministries, and ultimately their martyrdoms, were the tactical maneuvers by which Christ dismantled the spiritual strongholds of the Greco-Roman world.
