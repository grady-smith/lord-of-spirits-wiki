---
title: "Theophany"
tags: [concept]
---

A Theophany is a visible manifestation or appearance of God to humanity. In the Old Testament, these encounters often involve the "Angel of the Lord" or the "Word of the Lord" appearing to patriarchs and prophets, such as to Abraham at the oaks of Mamre or to Moses in the burning bush. These manifestations were intensely tangible, often involving sensory elements like fire, clouds, or human-like figures, demonstrating that the transcendent God interacts directly and visibly with the material world.

In Orthodox theology, particularly as highlighted in the *Lord of Spirits*, all Old Testament theophanies are understood as Christophanies—pre-incarnate appearances of the second person of the Holy Trinity, the Logos or Word of God. Because God the Father is fundamentally invisible and unapproachable, it is always the Son who reveals the Father to creation. This deeply integrates the Old and New Testaments, showing that the God who walked with Adam and spoke to Moses is the exact same person who took on human flesh as Jesus Christ.
