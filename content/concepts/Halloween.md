title: "Halloween"
tags: [concept]
---

Halloween, originally "All Hallows' Eve," is a holiday celebrated on October 31st, the evening before the Western Christian feast of All Hallows' Day (All Saints' Day). Its historical roots are complex, blending early Christian practices of commemorating saints and martyrs with elements derived from ancient Celtic harvest festivals, particularly Samhain, a time when the veil between the physical world and the spirit world was believed to be thin.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* and an Orthodox worldview, modern Halloween is often analyzed in light of its connection to the unseen realm and cultural engagements with death. While the Orthodox Church celebrates All Saints' Sunday at a different time (the week after Pentecost), discussions around Halloween often emphasize the reality of spiritual forces and the Christian necessity of facing death not with morbid fascination or fear, but with the triumphant hope of the Resurrection, asserting Christ's ultimate victory over the demonic powers.
