title: "Astyages"

Astyages was the last king of the Median Empire, ruling in the 6th century BC before being overthrown by his grandson, Cyrus the Great. In historical and biblical contexts, the transition of power from Astyages to Cyrus marked the end of Median dominance and the rise of the Achaemenid Persian Empire. This shift was profoundly significant for the Jewish people in exile, as Cyrus would ultimately issue the decree allowing them to return to Jerusalem and rebuild the Temple, an act prophesied by Isaiah.

Within the broader Ancient Near Eastern narrative and the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, the fall of Astyages and the rise of Cyrus underscore the sovereignty of the Most High God over the nations and their patron spiritual powers. God is seen as orchestrating the rise and fall of empires, utilizing rulers like Cyrus—regardless of their own theological understandings—as instruments to fulfill His divine purposes for Israel and the world. The events surrounding Astyages' defeat highlight the intersection of historical geopolitics and the unseen realm, where earthly kings are maneuvered as part of a larger cosmic plan.
