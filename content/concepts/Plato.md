---
title: "Plato"
tags: [concept]
---

Plato was a classical Greek philosopher born in Athens during the Classical period, foundational to Western philosophy. As a student of Socrates, he founded the Academy and authored philosophical dialogues that explored themes of justice, beauty, equality, and the distinction between the material world of becoming and the eternal world of forms or ideas.

In the *Lord of Spirits* and ancient Christian theology, Plato is often acknowledged as having intuited or borrowed elements of truth regarding the transcendent nature of reality. Early Church Fathers sometimes referred to him as a "Moses speaking Attic Greek." However, his stark dualism—which elevated the spiritual intellect while depreciating the material body—stands in contrast to the holistic biblical and Orthodox view of creation. Unlike Platonism, which seeks the soul's escape from the physical world, the Orthodox framework emphasizes the bodily resurrection and the ultimate transfiguration of all material creation.
