---
title: "Noah"
tags: [concept]
---

Noah is the patriarch chosen by God to survive the catastrophic global flood, tasked with building the Ark to preserve humanity and representatives of the animal kingdom. In the context of the ancient Near East and the *Lord of Spirits*, Noah functions as a new Adam, bridging the corrupted pre-flood world and the post-flood reality.

The flood narrative is not merely a story of divine anger against general human misbehavior; it is an account of cosmic spiritual warfare. The earth had been irredeemably corrupted by the illicit union of the Watchers and human women, producing the Nephilim (giants). The flood was the uncreation of the world, utilizing the chaotic waters to destroy the demonic hybrids and cleanse the earth of the bloodshed they caused. Noah, found righteous, is the seed preserved through the waters of chaos.

Upon leaving the Ark, Noah functions as a priest, offering a sacrifice that restores the relationship between heaven and earth. The covenant God makes with Noah, accompanied by the sign of the rainbow (God hanging up His weapon of war), establishes the boundaries of the post-flood world. Noah's subsequent planting of a vineyard also parallels Adam in the garden, reflecting both the restoration of humanity's role and its ongoing susceptibility to failure.
