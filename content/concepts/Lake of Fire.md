---
title: "Lake of Fire"
tags: [concept]
---

The Lake of Fire is the ultimate eschatological destination for the devil, his angels, and those who align with them. In Orthodox theology, it is not a torture chamber created by God, but rather the unmediated experience of God's holy presence.

God is a "consuming fire"; for the righteous, this fire is illuminating and purifying, but for the wicked and the rebellious spiritual powers, the exact same fire is experienced as burning and destruction. It represents the final cleansing of the cosmos from chaos and death.
