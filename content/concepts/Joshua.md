---
title: "Joshua"
tags: [concept]
---

Joshua, the successor to Moses, led the Israelite conquest of Canaan. In the worldview of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, this conquest is not merely a political or ethnic conflict, but a targeted campaign of spiritual warfare. It is understood as driving out the Nephilim tribes (the giant clans) and reclaiming the land from rebellious spiritual powers.

By executing the ban (herem) on specific giant-inhabited cities, Joshua was purging the land of the demonic seed and the localized worship of fallen angels. This established a purified sacred space for Yahweh's presence among His people.
