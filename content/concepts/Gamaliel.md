---
title: "Gamaliel"
tags: [concept]
---

Gamaliel the Elder was a highly respected Pharisee, a leading authority in the Sanhedrin, and a renowned teacher of the Law in the first century AD. He is famously identified in the Book of Acts as the teacher of the Apostle Paul (then Saul of Tarsus). According to Acts 5, Gamaliel intervened to save the apostles from execution, advising the council to leave them alone with the reasoning that if their movement was of human origin, it would fail, but if it was from God, it could not be overthrown.

In the study of Second Temple Judaism, Gamaliel represents the nuanced and diverse nature of rabbinic thought during that period. His moderate stance, characteristic of the school of Hillel, contrasts with the more zealous elements of his time. In Christian history, Gamaliel's advice is seen as an instrument of divine providence, preserving the early Church during its vulnerable infancy.
