---
title: "Dominions"
tags: [concept]
---
In the traditional Christian angelology articulated by figures like St. Dionysius the Areopagite, Dominions (or Dominations) refer to a specific rank or "choir" of angelic beings within the celestial hierarchy. They are generally grouped in the second sphere of angels, alongside Virtues and Powers. As their name suggests, they are associated with the administration of cosmic order, authority, and the regulation of lower angelic ranks.

Within the ancient cosmic framework often discussed on *The Lord of Spirits*, terms like Dominions, Principalities, and Powers were widely understood in the first century as the actual, conscious spiritual entities that governed the nations and the natural world. Many of these entities rebelled against God, becoming the corrupt "gods of the nations." When the Apostle Paul states that our struggle is against the "rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness" (Ephesians 6:12), he is referring directly to these fallen hierarchical beings, whose dominion was ultimately overthrown by Christ's crucifixion and resurrection.
