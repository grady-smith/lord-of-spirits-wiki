title: "Unction"
tags: [concept]
---

Holy Unction, or the Anointing of the Sick, is a sacrament (or mystery) in the Orthodox Church aimed at the healing of soul and body and the forgiveness of sins. Following the scriptural mandate found in James 5:14-15, priests anoint the faithful with blessed oil accompanied by prayers. It is not viewed merely as a "last rites" preparation for death, but as a profound therapeutic grace for all who are physically or spiritually ailing.

Within the worldview of the *Lord of Spirits*, illness and death are inextricably linked to the fallen state of creation and the influence of the demonic realm. Therefore, Holy Unction is an act of spiritual warfare. By anointing the sick with holy oil—which represents the presence and grace of the Holy Spirit—the Church is actively pushing back against the forces of decay and chaos, asserting the restorative power of Christ’s resurrection over the physical body and the soul.
