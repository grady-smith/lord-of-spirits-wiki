---
title: "Melchizedek"
tags: [concept]
---

Melchizedek is the mysterious king of Salem and "priest of God Most High" who suddenly appears in Genesis to bless Abraham. He offers bread and wine and receives a tithe from the patriarch.

In Orthodox theology and the Epistle to the Hebrews, Melchizedek is a vital typological figure—and sometimes understood as a Christophany (a pre-incarnate appearance of the Logos). His eternal, royal priesthood is vastly superior to the later Levitical priesthood, and it is the priesthood that Christ Himself eternally fulfills.
