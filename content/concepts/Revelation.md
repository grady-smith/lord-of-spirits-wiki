The Book of Revelation, also known as the Apocalypse of John, is the final book of the New Testament. Written by the Apostle John while exiled on the island of Patmos, it is a highly symbolic and apocalyptic vision of the heavenly liturgy, the judgment of the corrupt nations and their patron deities, and the ultimate consummation of the cosmos in the New Heavens and New Earth.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Revelation is read not as a chronological puzzle to predict modern geopolitical events, but as a profound theological disclosure of the Unseen Realm. It depicts the ongoing, active reality of the Divine Council, centered around the Lamb who was slain. The text vividly portrays the cosmic war between the Kingdom of God and the principalities and powers working through earthly empires (typified by "Babylon"), culminating in the final eviction of evil from God's creation and the permanent union of heaven and earth.
