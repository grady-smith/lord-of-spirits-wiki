title: "El Shaddai"
tags: [concept]
---
"El Shaddai" is a Hebrew name for God, traditionally translated as "God Almighty." Its exact ancient etymology is debated, but it is often linked to the Akkadian word *shadu* (mountain), suggesting the meaning "God of the Mountain." In the ancient Near East, mountains were profoundly significant as the cosmic dwelling places of the gods, the intersection between heaven and earth where divine decrees were issued.

In the patriarchal narratives of Genesis, God frequently reveals Himself to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as El Shaddai. Within the framework of *The Lord of Spirits* podcast, this title highlights God’s immovable, sovereign power and His presence in sacred, elevated spaces (like Mount Sinai and Mount Zion). Recognizing God as the "God of the Mountain" contrasts Him with the pagan deities of the ancient world, reinforcing that Yahweh is the true Lord of the cosmic mountain and the ultimate source of blessing, fertility, and protection for His covenant people.
