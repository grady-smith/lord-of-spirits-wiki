---
title: "Christophany"
tags: [concept]
---

A Christophany is an appearance or manifestation of the pre-incarnate Jesus Christ—the Second Person of the Trinity—in the Old Testament. The *Lord of Spirits* podcast places a major emphasis on the reality of Christophanies, arguing against the idea that the God of the Old Testament is an invisible, distant "Father" figure while Jesus only appears in the New. Instead, drawing on ancient Jewish "Two Powers in Heaven" theology, the podcast highlights that whenever God is seen, heard, or interacts directly with humanity in the Hebrew Bible, it is the Word (Logos) of God.

Prominent examples of Christophanies include the "Angel of the Lord" who speaks to Moses in the burning bush, the "Commander of the Lord's Army" who meets Joshua, and the figure sitting on the throne in Ezekiel's vision. These appearances establish the continuity of the divine identity; the same divine person who delivered Israel from Egypt and defeated the gods of Canaan is the one who later took on human flesh. Understanding Christophanies is essential to Orthodox theology, as it demonstrates that the Trinity is deeply embedded in the Hebrew scriptures and that Christ has always been the mediator between the unseen Father and creation.
