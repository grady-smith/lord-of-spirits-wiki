title: "Abraham"
tags: [concept]
---
Abraham is the foundational Patriarch of Israel, whose story begins in Genesis when Yahweh calls him out of Ur of the Chaldeans. In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and the ancient Near East, Abraham's calling is a direct response to the disaster of the Tower of Babel. While the nations of the world were disinherited and handed over to the dominion of lesser spiritual beings (the "gods of the nations," as per Deuteronomy 32:8-9), God chose Abraham to create a new, distinct nation that would serve as His own portion and the beachhead for reclaiming the world.

Abraham's journey is characterized by a definitive break from the Babylonian religious system, which was centered on the worship of the celestial bodies and local patron deities. Instead, Abraham establishes altars across Canaan, effectively claiming the territory for the Most High God (El Elyon). His interactions with God are deeply personal and frequently involve Christophanies—appearances of the pre-incarnate Christ, often identified as the "Angel of the Lord."

A pivotal moment in Orthodox theology is the "Hospitality of Abraham" at the Oak of Mamre, where Abraham is visited by three mysterious men. He addresses the three as one ("Lord"), a narrative that the Church Fathers and Orthodox iconography understand as a prefigurement or revelation of the Holy Trinity. Abraham's faithfulness, culminating in his willingness to sacrifice Isaac, cements his status as the ultimate model of faith and the father of all who belong to Christ.
