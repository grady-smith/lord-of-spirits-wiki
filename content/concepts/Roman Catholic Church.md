The Roman Catholic Church is the largest Christian church, tracing its origins to the original Apostolic community but distinguished by its belief in the universal jurisdiction and infallibility of the Bishop of Rome (the Pope). Following the Great Schism of 1054, the Roman Church developed distinct theological, liturgical, and administrative paradigms separate from the Eastern Orthodox Patriarchates.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the theology of the Roman Catholic Church is frequently contrasted with the older, shared consensus of the early Church Fathers. The podcast critiques the medieval scholastic developments within Catholicism—such as the legalistic framing of sin and merit, the concept of a created grace, the doctrine of Purgatory, and absolute papal supremacy—as departures from the more participatory, therapeutic, and cosmic warfare models maintained by Eastern Orthodoxy.
