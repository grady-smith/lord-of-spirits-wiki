title: "Christ"
tags: [concept]
---

"Christ" is not a surname, but a title derived from the Greek *Christos*, which translates the Hebrew *Mashiach* (Messiah), meaning "the Anointed One." In the Ancient Near East and Israelite tradition, prophets, priests, and kings were anointed with oil to consecrate them for their divine roles and equip them with the Spirit of God. Jesus of Nazareth is the Christ because He is the ultimate fulfillment of all three offices: the perfect Prophet who speaks God’s word, the perfect High Priest who offers Himself as the ultimate sacrifice, and the eternal King who reigns over the cosmos.

In the cosmology of the *Lord of Spirits* and Orthodox theology, the title of Christ is intimately tied to His role as the Divine Warrior and the second person of the Holy Trinity. As the Christ, He is the incarnate Logos who descends into the fallen world, assumes human nature to heal it, and engages in direct, cosmic combat with the rebellious spiritual powers. His "anointing" is not merely symbolic, but the literal indwelling of the fullness of the Godhead bodily, enabling Him to defeat death by death and restore humanity to its rightful place within the Divine Council.
