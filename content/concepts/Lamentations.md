title: "Lamentations"
tags: [concept]
---

The Book of Lamentations is a collection of poetic elegies mourning the destruction of Jerusalem and the Temple by the Babylonians in 586 BC.

In the prophetic worldview, this devastation was not a failure of Yahweh to protect His city, but the inevitable consequence of Judah's spiritual adultery. By aligning with foreign deities and participating in demonic rituals (like child sacrifice), the nation brought upon itself the covenantal curses and the withdrawal of God's protection.
