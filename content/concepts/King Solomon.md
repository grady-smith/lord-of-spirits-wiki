---
title: "King Solomon"
tags: [concept]
---

King Solomon, the son of David, is renowned for his wisdom and for building the First Temple in Jerusalem. The Temple was designed as an architectural embodiment of the cosmos and the divine council, where heaven and earth intersected.

While later traditions (like the Testament of Solomon) heavily associate him with the magical binding of demons, his biblical narrative ends tragically. By building high places for the foreign gods of his wives, Solomon reintroduced the worship of fallen spiritual beings into the heart of Israel.
