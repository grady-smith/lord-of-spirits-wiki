Father Stephen De Young is an Orthodox Christian priest, biblical scholar, and author, recognized for his deep expertise in the ancient Near East, Second Temple Judaism, and the biblical languages. He is the co-host of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and the author of several influential books, including *The Religion of the Apostles* and *God is a Man of War*.

As a scholar and pastor, Father Stephen's work focuses on recovering the ancient, enchanted worldview of the biblical authors. He frequently elucidates how early Christianity was not a departure from Second Temple Judaism but its fulfillment. His teachings consistently highlight the reality of the Divine Council, the presence of Christ throughout the Old Testament (Christophanies), and the profound spiritual dimensions of biblical narratives that have often been obscured by modern, rationalistic interpretations.
