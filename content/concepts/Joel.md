title: "Joel"
tags: [concept]
---
Joel is one of the twelve Minor Prophets of the Hebrew Bible, whose short but powerful book focuses intensely on the "Day of the Lord." The prophecy begins with a devastating plague of locusts and a severe drought in Judah, which Joel interprets as an immediate, physical manifestation of God's judgment and a harbinger of a far greater, cosmic judgment to come.

In the ancient prophetic worldview, the Day of the Lord is not merely the end of the world, but the decisive moment when Yahweh actively invades history to set things right—destroying his enemies, purging wickedness, and vindicating his faithful people. Joel urgently calls the nation to deep, communal repentance, fasting, and mourning in the temple to avert this wrath.

For Orthodox theology and the New Testament, Joel 2 contains one of the most critical prophecies of the new covenant: the promise that God will pour out His Spirit "on all flesh." The Apostle Peter explicitly quotes this passage on the day of Pentecost (Acts 2), declaring that the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the Apostles marks the inauguration of the final age. Through Pentecost, the prophetic calling and the direct presence of God are no longer limited to select individuals, but are democratized to the entire Church.
