---
title: "Eve"
tags: [concept]
---

Eve is the first woman in the biblical narrative, created by God from the rib (or side) of Adam to be his counterpart and partner. In ancient Near Eastern context, her creation signifies the completion of humanity as male and female, designed to co-rule over creation as God's image-bearers. The name Eve (Hawwah in Hebrew) is related to the word for "life," identifying her as the "mother of all the living."

In Orthodox theology, Eve's role in the Fall is often contrasted with that of the Virgin Mary, who is referred to as the "New Eve." Where the first Eve's disobedience brought death and exile from Eden, the New Eve's obedience ("Let it be done to me according to your word") brought forth the Incarnation and the reversal of the curse. This typological relationship highlights the profound interconnectedness of humanity's fall and redemption within the cosmic narrative.
