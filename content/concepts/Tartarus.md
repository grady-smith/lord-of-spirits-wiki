title: "Tartarus"
tags: [concept]
---

Tartarus is a term derived from Greek mythology, originally designating a deep, gloomy abyss used as a dungeon of torment and a prison for the Titans. In the Hellenistic period, the concept was adapted into Jewish apocalyptic literature to describe the deepest, darkest region of the underworld. Unlike Hades or Sheol, which were considered the general abode of the human dead, Tartarus was specifically understood as a place of confinement for rebellious spiritual beings and the profoundly wicked.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Tartarus is primarily discussed in relation to 2 Peter 2:4, which states that God "cast down to hell [Tartarus] and delivered into chains of darkness" the angels who sinned. This is a direct reference to the Watchers of Genesis 6 and the Book of Enoch. By utilizing the term Tartarus, St. Peter is anchoring the Christian understanding of spiritual warfare in the Second Temple tradition: the rebellious divine beings who corrupted humanity have been decisively judged and imprisoned, awaiting the final consummation of their defeat at the Last Judgment.
