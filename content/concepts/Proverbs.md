title: "Proverbs"
tags: [concept]
---

The Book of Proverbs is a central text of biblical wisdom literature, traditionally associated with King Solomon. It consists of collections of moral and philosophical maxims designed to instruct the reader—often framed as a father advising his son—in the ways of righteousness, prudence, and the fear of the Lord, which is described as the beginning of wisdom.

In the theological landscape of the *Lord of Spirits*, Proverbs is significant for its personification of Wisdom (Holy Wisdom or *Hagia Sophia*). In chapters like Proverbs 8, Wisdom is described as being present with God before the creation of the world, acting as a master craftsman at His side. In early Christian theology, this personified Wisdom was intimately identified with the second person of the Trinity, the *Logos* or Word of God, establishing that Christ is the eternal, active wisdom through whom the cosmos was ordered and sustained.
