The Enlightenment, or the Age of Reason, was an intellectual and cultural movement in 17th and 18th century Europe that heavily emphasized reason, individualism, and empirical science over tradition and religious authority. It gave rise to deism—the belief in a clockmaker God who created the universe but does not intervene in its natural laws—and eventually paved the way for modern secular materialism.

In the critical framework of *The Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Enlightenment is viewed as a catastrophic rupture in how humanity perceives the cosmos. By rejecting the ancient, sacramental worldview of a universe suffused with spiritual presence (angels, demons, and God's uncreated energies), Enlightenment thought "demythologized" reality. This shift reduced the cosmos to dead, mechanical matter and relegated religion to a mere system of private ethics or intellectual propositions. The Orthodox Church pushes back against this framework, insisting that the ancient, enchanted worldview of the Scriptures is not primitive superstition, but an accurate description of a deeply spiritual reality.
