Andrew, the Apostle and brother of Peter, holds the title of the "First-Called" (Protokletos) in Orthodox tradition, as he was the first disciple to follow Jesus after being directed by John the Baptist. In the framework of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the calling of the Apostles is understood as the formation of the new Divine Council on earth, chosen by Christ to assist in His cosmic reclamation of the nations.

Following Pentecost, the Apostles were dispatched across the known world to directly confront the territorial spirits that had ruled the gentile nations since the Tower of Babel. According to ancient Church tradition, St. Andrew’s apostolic mission took him to the regions around the Black Sea, Scythia, and ultimately to Greece. His preaching and establishing of local churches were acts of spiritual warfare, planting outposts of the Kingdom of God in territories previously dominated by pagan deities.

Andrew ultimately suffered martyrdom by crucifixion in the Greek city of Patras on an X-shaped cross. In the early Christian worldview, martyrdom was not a defeat, but the ultimate victory and a direct participation in Christ's triumph over the powers of death and Hades. Through his witness and death, Andrew struck a decisive blow against the demonic principalities of the regions he evangelized.
