---
title: "Chrismation"
tags: [concept]
---

Chrismation is the Orthodox sacrament (mystery) that immediately follows Baptism, corresponding to what is known as Confirmation in the West. It involves the anointing of the newly baptized with Holy Chrism (Myrrh)—a specially prepared and consecrated oil—upon various parts of the body, accompanied by the words "The seal of the gift of the Holy Spirit." This sacrament enacts the personal Pentecost of the individual, conferring the indwelling presence of the Holy Spirit and fully incorporating them into the life of the Church.

In the cosmic and spiritual warfare framework highlighted by the *Lord of Spirits*, Chrismation is understood as an ordination. In the Old Testament, prophets, priests, and kings were anointed with oil to signify their empowerment by God for a specific role. Through Chrismation, every Christian is anointed to become a participant in Christ's royal priesthood. The "seal" of Chrismation physically marks the believer as God's exclusive property, a dedicated temple of the Holy Spirit, equipped with spiritual armor to engage in the ongoing battle against the fallen principalities and powers in the world.
