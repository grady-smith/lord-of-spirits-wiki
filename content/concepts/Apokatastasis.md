---
title: "Apokatastasis"
tags: [concept]
---
*Apokatastasis* is a Greek term meaning the "restoration of all things." In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast and Orthodox theology, it is a highly nuanced eschatological concept. In some historical contexts (most notably associated with Origen), it was used to argue for absolute universalism—the idea that every single being, including Satan and the demons, will eventually be saved and reconciled to God. The Church explicitly condemned this specific formulation as heresy.

However, there is an Orthodox understanding of *apokatastasis* that is profoundly biblical, rooted in passages like Acts 3:21. It refers to the ultimate healing and reordering of the cosmos. When Christ returns, death will be entirely destroyed, the dead will be physically resurrected, and the entire material creation will be transfigured and freed from the bondage of corruption.

In this sense, "all things" are indeed restored to their proper, God-ordained order. The rebellious spiritual powers are finally and completely subjected under the feet of Christ, and the cosmos functions perfectly as a temple of the Holy Spirit. For those who love God, this restored reality is Paradise; for those humans and demons who remain adamantly opposed to God, this exact same inescapable reality of divine order and presence is experienced as the eternal fire of Gehenna.
