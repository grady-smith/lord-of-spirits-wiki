---
title: "Mary"
tags: [concept]
---

Mary is the Mother of God (Theotokos), the young Jewish woman chosen to bear the incarnate Word. In Orthodox theology, her obedience and "let it be" reverses the disobedience of Eve, making her the New Eve.

She is venerated as the fulfillment of the Ark of the Covenant, as she physically contained the uncontainable fire of the Godhead. In the heavenly realm, she holds a preeminent place in the divine council as the Queen Mother, seated at the right hand of her Son.
