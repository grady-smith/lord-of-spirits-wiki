---
title: "Jesus Christ"
tags: [concept]
---
"Jesus Christ" is not a first and last name, but a name and a profound theological title. "Jesus" means "Yahweh saves," and "Christ" is the Greek translation (*Christos*) of the Hebrew *Mashiach* (Messiah), meaning "the Anointed One." To confess Jesus as the Christ is to declare Him as the fulfillment of all Old Testament prophecies, the ultimate King, High Priest, and Prophet anointed by the Holy Spirit.

In Orthodox theology and the cosmic worldview of the *Lord of Spirits*, Jesus Christ is the Creator who stepped into His own creation. He is the visible Yahweh of the Old Testament—the Angel of the Lord who appeared to the Patriarchs, spoke to Moses in the burning bush, and led Israel through the wilderness. In the Incarnation, this eternal Word assumed a complete human nature to wage war against the forces of darkness.

Through His life, crucifixion, descent into Hades, and resurrection, Jesus Christ decisively conquered death and the devil. He did not merely offer a moral example or satisfy an angry deity's wrath; He bound the "strong man" (Satan) and plundered his house, rescuing humanity from the power of the grave. As the resurrected and ascended Lord, Jesus Christ now rules over the cosmos, uniting heaven and earth in Himself and offering eternal life (*theosis*) to all who join His Body, the Church.
