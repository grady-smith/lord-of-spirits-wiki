---
title: "Ecumenical Council"
tags: [concept]
---
An Ecumenical Council is a major gathering of bishops from across the entire Christian world to resolve theological controversies and establish dogmatic consensus. The Eastern Orthodox Church formally recognizes seven such councils, beginning with the First Council of Nicaea (325 AD) and concluding with the Second Council of Nicaea (787 AD). These councils addressed vital issues regarding the Holy Trinity, the nature of Christ (Christology), and the veneration of icons.

In the framework of *The Lord of Spirits*, Ecumenical Councils are not merely administrative or political meetings; they are the earthly reflection and participation in the heavenly Divine Council. In the ancient Near East, the divine assembly deliberated and issued decrees governing the cosmos. The Holy Spirit guides the bishops in council to articulate the truth of the Gospel against the lies of heretical movements (which are often understood as doctrines of demons). Thus, the decrees of an Ecumenical Council are binding because they represent the unified, conciliar voice of the Church speaking with the authority of Christ, protecting the flock from spiritual deception.
