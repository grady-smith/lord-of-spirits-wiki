---
title: "Kings"
tags: [concept]
---

The Books of Kings detail the history of the Israelite monarchy from Solomon to the Babylonian exile. It traces the division of the kingdom and the systemic failures of its rulers.

The central theological thread of Kings is the constant struggle between the exclusive worship of Yahweh and the pervasive idolatry (the worship of Baal, Asherah, and other fallen beings) that ultimately led to the withdrawal of God's presence and the destruction of the nation.
