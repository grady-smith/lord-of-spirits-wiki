The Neolithic era, or the New Stone Age, represents the final stage of cultural evolution or technological development among prehistoric humans, characterized by the transition from nomadic hunter-gatherer lifestyles to settled agriculture, the domestication of animals, and the establishment of the first cities.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the developments of the Neolithic era are often discussed in correlation with the early chapters of Genesis, particularly the lineage of Cain and the narratives surrounding the pre-flood world. The sudden emergence of metallurgy, complex urbanization, and sophisticated warfare in human history is linked, in ancient Jewish and early Christian thought, to illicit angelic pedagogy. According to the Enochic tradition, the rebellious Watchers taught humans the secrets of war, sorcery, and seduction, accelerating human civilization towards violence and corruption.

Therefore, the Neolithic transition is not viewed purely as a neutral scientific progression, but as a period deeply entangled with spiritual rebellion. The foundation of the first cities (beginning with Enoch, built by Cain) and the rise of civilization are often depicted as humanity’s attempt to secure its own survival and autonomy independently of God, heavily influenced by demonic forces that sought to plunge the creation into chaos.
