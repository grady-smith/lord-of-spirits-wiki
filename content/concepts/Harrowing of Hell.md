---
title: "Harrowing of Hell"
tags: [concept]
---

The Harrowing of Hell, or the Descent into Hades, is the triumphant event following the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, where His soul descended into the underworld (Hades/Sheol). In Orthodox theology, this is not a passive waiting period, but a dynamic, cosmic victory. Christ entered Hades as the Lord of Life, shattering the gates of brass, breaking the chains of the captives, and liberating the righteous dead—beginning with Adam and Eve—from the tyranny of death.

This event is the focal point of the Orthodox celebration of Pascha (Easter) and is vividly depicted in traditional resurrection iconography. In the framework of the *Lord of Spirits*, the Harrowing of Hell represents the ultimate defeat of the rebellious spiritual powers that held humanity hostage. By willingly entering the realm of the dead, the divine life of Christ destroyed death from the inside out, fundamentally altering the cosmos and opening the path to eternal life.
