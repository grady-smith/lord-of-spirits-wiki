---
title: "Liturgy"
tags: [concept]
---

Liturgy, meaning the "work of the people," refers primarily to the Divine Liturgy in the Orthodox Church. It is not just an earthly gathering, but an actual participation in the heavenly worship of the Divine Council.

When the Church gathers for Liturgy, the boundaries between heaven and earth become permeable. The faithful join with the angels, archangels, and saints in offering the Eucharistic sacrifice, entering directly into the uncreated presence of God.
