---
title: "Archangel Gabriel"
tags: [concept]
---
The Archangel Gabriel, whose name translates to "God is my strength," is one of the highest-ranking spiritual beings in the Divine Council. In both the biblical text and Orthodox tradition, Gabriel functions primarily as the chief messenger who reveals God's most critical apocalyptic and salvific plans.

In the *Lord of Spirits* context, Gabriel is prominent in the Book of Daniel, where he pulls back the curtain on the spiritual warfare occurring behind earthly geopolitical conflicts, revealing the timeline for the coming of the Messiah. However, his most significant role is in the New Testament, where he serves as the herald of the Incarnation. He announces the birth of John the Baptist to Zechariah and delivers the Annunciation to the Virgin Mary.

Because words and presence carry profound power in the ancient Near Eastern worldview, Gabriel's announcements are not merely informational; they are performative acts that initiate the long-awaited invasion of God into the material world. By announcing that Mary will conceive the Son of the Most High, Gabriel signals the beginning of the end for the rebellious powers of darkness, acting as the loyal herald of the true King.
