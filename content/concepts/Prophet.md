---
title: "Prophet"
tags: [concept]
---

A prophet in the biblical tradition is a person chosen by God to serve as a messenger and covenant mediator. Unlike priests, who inherited their roles and maintained the established liturgy and sacred space, prophets were often called spontaneously from various backgrounds to disrupt the status quo, confront idolatry, and call Israel back to faithfulness to Yahweh.

In the theology of the *Lord of Spirits*, a defining characteristic of a true prophet is participation in the Divine Council. Prophets are individuals whose spiritual eyes have been opened to the unseen realm, allowing them to hear the deliberations of God and His holy ones. They stand in the gap between the divine assembly and humanity, acting as God's prosecuting attorneys when the covenant is broken, and as heralds of restoration when repentance occurs. In Christ, the prophetic office finds its absolute fulfillment, as He is the very Word of God who perfectly reveals the Father.
