---
title: "1 John"
tags: [concept]
---
The First Epistle of John is a key New Testament text that directly addresses the reality of spiritual warfare and the necessity of the Incarnation. In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, 1 John is frequently highlighted for its clear declaration that "the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil." This portrays Christ's mission not merely as a transaction to forgive sins, but as a cosmic rescue operation to defeat the spiritual tyrants who had enslaved humanity.

The epistle strongly warns against the spirit of the Antichrist and emphasizes the necessity of testing the spirits. In the ancient world, interaction with the spiritual realm was assumed, but 1 John insists that any spirit that does not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is not from God. This was a critical polemic against early Gnostic-like movements (such as Docetism) that believed matter was evil and denied the physical reality of the Incarnation.

For Orthodox theology, 1 John reinforces the sacramental and embodied nature of salvation. By becoming truly human, the Logos sanctified the material world. The text calls believers to live in the light, which means active participation in the life of God and breaking fellowship with the dark powers through righteous living and love for the brethren. This active striving is framed as participation in Christ's ongoing victory over the rulers of this age.
