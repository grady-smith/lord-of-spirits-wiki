title: "Silouan the Athonite"
tags: [concept]
---

Saint Silouan the Athonite (1866–1938) was a Russian Orthodox monk who lived on Mount Athos in Greece. Born Simeon Ivanovich Antonov to a peasant family, he experienced a profound vision of Christ early in his monastic life, which granted him a taste of uncreated grace. However, he subsequently experienced a long period of spiritual darkness and the withdrawal of this grace, leading to intense spiritual warfare against despair and demonic attacks. 

In the context of Orthodox spirituality and themes often discussed on the *Lord of Spirits*, St. Silouan is renowned for his teaching on the acquisition of the Holy Spirit and the necessity of radical humility. During a period of intense demonic assault, Christ revealed to him the spiritual axiom: "Keep thy mind in hell, and despair not." This phrase encapsulates the Orthodox ascetic practice of profound self-condemnation and humility, recognizing one's own sinfulness as worthy of hell, while simultaneously clinging with absolute trust to the merciful love of God, thereby defeating the pride of the demons.

St. Silouan's teachings, preserved and published by his disciple Elder Sophrony (Sakharov), also emphasize profound love for enemies as the ultimate criterion of true Christian faith and the presence of the Holy Spirit. He taught that the whole world is interconnected, and the salvation of the individual is bound up with the salvation of all; to pray for the world, he said, is to shed blood. He was canonized by the Ecumenical Patriarchate in 1987.
