The Declaration of Independence is the foundational document of the United States, drafted primarily by Thomas Jefferson in 1776, which articulated the colonies' separation from the British Empire on the grounds of inherent, unalienable rights granted by a "Creator." While highly venerated in American civic religion, its philosophical underpinnings are deeply rooted in the Enlightenment and the deistic thought of figures like John Locke and Jean-Jacques Rousseau.

Within the critical framework of *The Lord of Spirits* podcast, the worldview surrounding documents like the Declaration of Independence represents a significant theological shift toward secularism and modernity. Rather than the ancient understanding of the cosmos—where nations were governed by spiritual beings (the Divine Council) under Yahweh's ultimate sovereignty—Enlightenment philosophy posited a clockmaker God who is distant and uninvolved. Consequently, the modern nation-state replaces the ancient "gods of the nations" with abstract concepts like "The State," "The Market," or "The People," which subtly function as new, secularized principalities demanding ultimate allegiance and sacrifice.
