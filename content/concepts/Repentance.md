---
title: "Repentance"
tags: [concept]
---

In the biblical context, repentance (*metanoia* in Greek) literally means a "change of mind" or "turning around." It is not merely an emotional experience of guilt or a psychological realization, but an active, physical reorientation of one's life away from sin and idolatry, turning back toward faithfulness to Yahweh and His covenant.

Within Orthodox theology and the *Lord of Spirits*, repentance is understood functionally as realigning oneself with the divine order. It involves turning away from the destructive influence of the fallen spiritual powers—the "rulers of this age"—and re-entering communion with the true God. This is an ongoing, therapeutic process of healing and purification, rather than a one-time forensic legal transaction. It is the fundamental prerequisite for participating in the life of the Holy Trinity and experiencing the ongoing transfiguration of the soul.
