---
title: "Jude"
tags: [concept]
---

The Epistle of Jude is a short New Testament letter that heavily draws on the worldview of Second Temple Judaism. It famously quotes directly from 1 Enoch and references the rebellion of the angels who "left their proper domain."

In Orthodox theology, Jude underscores the continuity of the ancient spiritual conflict. It serves as a reminder that the apostolic church operated fully within an enchanted worldview, where the cosmic rebellion of spiritual powers and their impending judgment by Christ were central to the gospel message.
