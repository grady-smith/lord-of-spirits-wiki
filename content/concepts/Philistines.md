The Philistines were an ancient people who settled on the southern coast of Canaan around the 12th century BC, likely part of the broader migration of the "Sea Peoples" from the Aegean region. They became one of the primary adversaries of ancient Israel during the period of the Judges and the early monarchy, famous for their iron weaponry, organized city-states (the Pentapolis), and champions like Goliath.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Philistines are not merely historical geopolitical rivals but represent the ongoing manifestation of the Nephilim lineage and the chaotic forces arrayed against God's order. Figures such as Goliath are frequently identified with the remnant of the giant clans (the Rephaim/Anakim), embodying the corrupted, hybrid lineage associated with the fallen Watchers. The conflicts between David and the Philistines are thus understood not just as terrestrial border skirmishes, but as active chapters in the cosmic war between Yahweh and the demonic forces seeking to thwart His purposes.
