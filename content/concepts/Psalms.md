The Book of Psalms, or the Psalter, is a collection of 150 ancient Israelite hymns, prayers, and liturgical poems, many of which are traditionally attributed to King David. The book serves as the master prayer book of the Bible, covering the full spectrum of human experience before God and structured conceptually around the history of Israel, the Davidic covenant, and the eschatological kingship of Yahweh.

In the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, the Psalms are deeply mined for their rich cosmology and theology of the unseen realm. Texts like Psalm 82 (God standing in the divine council) and Psalm 74 (the defeat of Leviathan) provide the essential vocabulary for the podcast's exploration of spiritual warfare. In the Orthodox tradition, the Psalms are chanted continuously, viewed as prophetic texts that reveal the incarnation, suffering, and ultimate cosmic triumph of Jesus Christ over the rebellious spiritual powers.
