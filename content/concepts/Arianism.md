title: "Arianism"
tags: [concept]
---
Arianism was a severe theological heresy that arose in the early 4th century, spearheaded by a priest named Arius. The core claim of Arianism was that Jesus Christ, the Son of God, was not co-eternal with God the Father, but was rather a created being—the first and greatest of all creations, but nevertheless a creature ("there was a time when he was not").

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, Arianism is devastating because it fundamentally destroys the narrative of the cosmic war. In the ancient Near Eastern worldview, there is a strict ontological divide between the uncreated Creator (Yahweh) and all created spiritual beings (the angels, the Divine Council). If Christ is merely a created being, then He is simply another angel, and His incarnation and crucifixion are not the direct invasion of the Most High God to rescue humanity, but merely God sending a subordinate to do the work.

Orthodox theology, decisively formulated against Arianism at the Council of Nicaea (325 AD), insists that Christ is *homoousios* (of the exact same essence) with the Father. He is the uncreated Lord of the Divine Council. Only because He is truly God can His descent into Hades and His victory over death definitively shatter the power of the rebellious gods and accomplish the salvation and theosis of humanity.
