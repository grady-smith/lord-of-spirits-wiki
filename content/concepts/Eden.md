---
title: "Eden"
tags: [concept]
---
In biblical theology, Eden is not merely an idyllic terrestrial garden, but the primordial sanctuary and the original meeting place between God and humanity. In the ancient Near Eastern context, gardens upon mountains were the dwelling places of the gods. Eden is described as a well-watered, elevated cosmic mountain where Yahweh’s Divine Council gathered, and where Adam and Eve were placed as royal priests to tend the sanctuary and expand its borders to the rest of the world.

Within the *Lord of Spirits* paradigm, the Fall in Eden was a cosmic disaster orchestrated by a rebellious spiritual being (the serpent), resulting in humanity's exile from the divine presence and the introduction of death. Consequently, the entirety of salvation history is the process of restoring and glorifying Eden. The Tabernacle, the Temple, and ultimately the Orthodox Christian Church are all theological continuations of Eden—sacred spaces where God dwells with man, culminating in the eschatological vision of the New Jerusalem, which is depicted as the ultimate, eternal Eden.
