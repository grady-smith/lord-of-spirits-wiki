title: "St. John of Damascus"
tags: [concept]
---

In Orthodox theology, St. John of Damascus (c. 675–749) is a pivotal Church Father known for his systematic articulation of the Christian faith, most notably in *An Exact Exposition of the Orthodox Faith*. He is celebrated as a great defender of the veneration of icons during the Iconoclastic Controversy. St. John argued that because the invisible God became flesh and assumed a material body in Jesus Christ, it is not only permissible but essential to depict Him using material means, as the Incarnation fundamentally changes the relationship between God and the material world.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits* podcast, St. John of Damascus is frequently referenced regarding his rich demonology and angelology, which synthesize scriptural data and earlier patristic thought. He famously structured the angelic ranks into the nine choirs that remain standard in Orthodox tradition. Furthermore, his teachings on the spiritual world consistently emphasize the participatory nature of creation, where material elements and spiritual beings alike are designed to participate in and reflect the glory of the Holy Trinity.
