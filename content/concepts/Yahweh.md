title: "Yahweh"
tags: [concept]
---

Yahweh (often represented in English Bibles as LORD) is the personal covenant name of the God of Israel, revealed to Moses at the burning bush. The name is related to the Hebrew verb "to be," indicating God's absolute, self-existent, and uncreated nature ("I AM WHO I AM"). In the context of ancient Near Eastern polytheism, Yahweh is revealed not merely as a regional deity, but as the Most High God, the uncreated Creator who stands completely above and apart from the created spiritual beings of the Divine Council.

The *Lord of Spirits* podcast extensively explores the "Two Powers in Heaven" tradition regarding Yahweh. In the Old Testament, Yahweh exists simultaneously as the transcendent, invisible Father in heaven, and as the visible, localized "Angel of the Lord" or "Word" walking on the earth. The Orthodox theological claim is that Jesus of Nazareth is the incarnate visible Yahweh of the Old Testament. Therefore, the entire narrative of the Bible is the story of Yahweh reclaiming His creation from the rebellious lesser gods.
