title: "Protestant"
tags: [concept]
---

A Protestant is an adherent of any of the Christian bodies that separated from the Church of Rome during the Reformation in the 16th century, or any group descended from them. Generally, Protestants hold to foundational principles such as *Sola Scriptura* (Scripture as the sole infallible authority) and *Sola Fide* (justification by faith alone), while rejecting the papal supremacy and various medieval sacramental developments of the Roman Catholic Church.

In the context of the *Lord of Spirits*, Protestant theological paradigms are frequently examined and critiqued for having inadvertently adopted the mechanistic and legalistic frameworks of the medieval Western Church. The podcast often contrasts Protestant models of salvation—such as penal substitutionary atonement and strict forensic justification—with the participatory, therapeutic, and cosmic warfare models preserved in Eastern Orthodox theology, emphasizing that modern Protestantism often operates with a significantly demythologized view of the spiritual realm.
