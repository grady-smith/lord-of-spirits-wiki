---
title: "Mother of God"
tags: [concept]
---

The title "Mother of God" (Greek: *Theotokos*, meaning "God-bearer") is the definitive theological title given to the Virgin Mary by the Council of Ephesus in 431 AD. It asserts the fundamental Christological truth that the person Mary gave birth to was not a mere human who was later united with God, but the eternal, uncreated Son of God who took on human flesh.

In the *Lord of Spirits* and ancient Orthodox tradition, the Mother of God holds a supreme place in the heavenly hierarchy, fulfilling the Old Testament typology of the Queen Mother (*Gebirah*). In the Davidic kingdom, the king's mother held an authoritative intercessory role at the king's right hand. As Christ is the eternal Davidic King, Mary takes her place as the Queen Mother of the cosmos, actively interceding for the faithful in the Divine Council.

Furthermore, she is understood as the ultimate fulfillment of the Ark of the Covenant and the Burning Bush—a created being who contained the uncontainable fire of the Divine Presence without being consumed. By her obedience, she reversed the disobedience of Eve, participating directly in the defeat of death and the demonic powers, and is honored by Orthodox Christians as "more honorable than the Cherubim and beyond compare more glorious than the Seraphim."
