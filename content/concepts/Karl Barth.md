---
title: "Karl Barth"
tags: [concept]
---

Karl Barth was a highly influential 20th-century Swiss Reformed theologian. While renowned for his massive dogmatic works and emphasis on the transcendence of God, his theology remains rooted in Western Protestant paradigms.

In discussions of Orthodox theology, figures like Barth are sometimes referenced to highlight the contrast between modern Western systematic theology and the ancient, participatory, and mystical worldview of the early Church and the ancient Near East.
