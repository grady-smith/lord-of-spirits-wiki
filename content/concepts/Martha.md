---
title: "Martha"
tags: [concept]
---

Martha was the sister of Lazarus and Mary of Bethany. While often remembered for being "anxious and troubled about many things" in her hospitality, her theological confession in the Gospel of John is profound.

Before the raising of Lazarus, Martha confesses Jesus as the Christ, the Son of God, and affirms her belief in the bodily resurrection on the last day. Her interaction with Christ highlights the deep, intimate relationship between the Incarnate God and His followers, leading to the conquest of death.
