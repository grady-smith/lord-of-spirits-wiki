---
title: "Obadiah"
tags: [concept]
---

Obadiah is the shortest book in the Old Testament, consisting of a single prophetic vision concerning the nation of Edom. Edom, located south of Judah, was founded by the descendants of Esau, the twin brother of Jacob (Israel). The prophecy fiercely condemns Edom for its pride and for violently betraying Judah during the Babylonian destruction of Jerusalem.

In the worldview of ancient Israel and the *Lord of Spirits*, prophecies against foreign nations are declarations of war against the spiritual entities that rule them. Edom is geographically and spiritually associated with Mount Seir, a region often linked to the demonic and the chaotic wilderness. By allying with Babylon to destroy Yahweh's temple and people, Edom aligned itself with the rebellious cosmic powers against the Most High God.

Obadiah's prophecy concludes with the promise that "the kingdom shall be the Lord's." This signifies that the ultimate result of the Day of the Lord will be the subjugation of the hostile nations and their spiritual rulers, restoring the correct cosmic order and establishing Yahweh's absolute sovereignty over all peoples, including the descendants of Esau.
