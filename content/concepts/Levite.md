---
title: "Levite"
tags: [concept]
---

A Levite is a member of the Israelite tribe of Levi, set apart by God for the service of the Tabernacle and later the Temple. They were chosen after the incident of the Golden Calf due to their zeal for Yahweh.

In the cosmic worldview of the Bible, Levites served as the earthly counterpart to the angelic guards (like the Cherubim) of God's heavenly throne room. Their primary duty was to guard the sacred space and protect the congregation from the lethal holiness of God's unmediated presence.
