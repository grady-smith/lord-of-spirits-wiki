---
title: "Malachi"
tags: [concept]
---

Malachi is the final prophetic book of the Old Testament. It addresses the post-exilic community in Jerusalem, castigating the priesthood for their corruption and the people for their faithlessness.

Crucially, Malachi prophesies the coming day of the Lord, a day of burning purification. He foretells the sending of "Elijah the prophet" to prepare the way, signaling the end of the old covenant era and setting the stage for the arrival of the Messiah to cleanse His temple.
