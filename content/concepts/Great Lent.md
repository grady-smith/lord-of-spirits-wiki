title: "Great Lent"
tags: [concept]
---

Great Lent is the primary season of preparation in the Orthodox Christian liturgical year, preceding the feast of Pascha (Easter). Lasting forty days (plus Holy Week), it is a period characterized by rigorous fasting, intensified prayer, almsgiving, and repentance. The structure of Great Lent is designed to mirror the forty days Christ spent fasting in the wilderness, as well as the forty years the Israelites spent journeying to the Promised Land.

In the worldview discussed in the *Lord of Spirits*, Great Lent is fundamentally a period of spiritual warfare and ascetical struggle. It is a time when the faithful intentionally strip away worldly comforts to confront their own passions and the demonic forces that seek to enslave them. The Lenten journey is a deliberate, communal exodus out of the "Egypt" of sin, preparing the believer to fully participate in the victorious death and resurrection of Christ at Pascha.
